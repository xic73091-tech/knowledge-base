# Oceanography

## Overview

Oceanography is the scientific study of the oceans, encompassing their physical, chemical, geological, and biological properties and processes. As Earth's oceans cover about 71% of the planet's surface and play critical roles in climate regulation, carbon cycling, and supporting biodiversity, oceanography is essential for understanding Earth systems. The field combines physics, chemistry, biology, and geology to investigate ocean currents, marine ecosystems, seafloor geology, chemical cycles, and the ocean's interactions with the atmosphere and climate.
