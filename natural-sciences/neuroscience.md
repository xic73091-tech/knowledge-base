# Neuroscience

## Overview

Neuroscience is the scientific study of the nervous system — from the molecular mechanisms of individual neurons to the complex circuits underlying cognition, emotion, and behavior. It bridges biology, psychology, medicine, and engineering, and is one of the fastest-growing fields in science.

## Core Concepts

### Cellular Neuroscience
- **Neurons**: Signal-transmitting cells; dendrites (input), cell body (integration), axon (output), synapses (communication)
- **Glial Cells**: Astrocytes (support, blood-brain barrier), oligodendrocytes (myelination), microglia (immune defense)
- **Action Potentials**: Electrical signals propagated along axons via voltage-gated ion channels; all-or-nothing principle
- **Synaptic Transmission**: Neurotransmitter release (dopamine, serotonin, GABA, glutamate); excitatory vs inhibitory synapses; synaptic plasticity

### Systems Neuroscience
- **Sensory Systems**: Visual (V1-V5), auditory, somatosensory, olfactory, gustatory processing pathways
- **Motor Systems**: Motor cortex, basal ganglia, cerebellum; voluntary vs involuntary movement
- **Limbic System**: Amygdala (emotion), hippocampus (memory), hypothalamus (homeostasis)
- **Prefrontal Cortex**: Executive function, decision-making, working memory, planning
- **Autonomic Nervous System**: Sympathetic (fight-or-flight) vs parasympathetic (rest-and-digest)

### Cognitive Neuroscience
- **Attention**: Selective, sustained, divided; neural correlates (frontoparietal network)
- **Memory Systems**: Episodic (hippocampus), procedural (basal ganglia), working (prefrontal), semantic (distributed)
- **Language**: Broca's area (production), Wernicke's area (comprehension); lateralization
- **Decision-Making**: Reward prediction (dopamine system), risk assessment, value computation
- **Consciousness**: Neural correlates of consciousness (NCC); global workspace theory; integrated information theory

### Developmental Neuroscience
- **Neurulation**: Neural tube formation; brain regionalization
- **Neurogenesis**: Birth of neurons; largely complete before birth except hippocampus and olfactory bulb
- **Synaptogenesis & Pruning**: Massive synapse formation followed by experience-dependent elimination
- **Critical Periods**: Windows of heightened plasticity for sensory, language, and social development
- **Myelination**: Progressive insulation of axons; continues into mid-20s (prefrontal cortex last)

### Molecular & Cellular Mechanisms
- **Neurotransmitter Systems**: Dopamine (reward, movement), serotonin (mood), acetylcholine (memory, attention), norepinephrine (arousal)
- **Receptor Pharmacology**: Ionotropic (fast) vs metabotropic (slow) receptors; agonists and antagonists
- **Signal Transduction**: Second messenger cascades; gene expression changes underlying long-term plasticity
- **Neuroplasticity**: Long-term potentiation (LTP) and long-term depression (LTD); structural and functional reorganization

### Computational Neuroscience
- **Neural Coding**: Rate coding vs temporal coding; population coding
- **Spiking Neural Networks**: Biologically realistic neuron models (Hodgkin-Huxley, integrate-and-fire)
- **Bayesian Brain**: Brain as inference machine; predictive processing
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Dopamine prediction error signals mirror temporal difference learning

### Research Methods
- **Neuroimaging**: fMRI (spatial resolution), EEG/MEG (temporal resolution), PET (molecular), diffusion tensor imaging (connectivity)
- **Electrophysiology**: Single-unit recording, multi-electrode arrays, patch clamp
- **Optogenetics**: Light-controlled activation/inhibition of specific neuron types
- **Brain Stimulation**: TMS (non-invasive), DBS (deep brain stimulation for Parkinson's)
- **Computational Modeling**: Simulating neural circuits; machine learning models of brain function

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Hebbian Learning | "Neurons that fire together wire together" — synaptic strengthening through co-activation |
| Connectionism | Cognitive functions emerge from networks of simple processing units |
| Global Workspace Theory | Consciousness arises from global broadcast of information across cortical networks |
| Predictive Processing | Brain constantly generates predictions and updates based on prediction errors |

## Important Figures

- **Santiago Ramón y Cajal**: Father of modern neuroscience; neuron doctrine
- **Charles Sherrington**: Synapse concept; reflex arcs
- **Alan Hodgkin & Andrew Huxley**: Action potential mechanism
- **Eric Kandel**: Molecular basis of memory (Aplysia)
- **Patricia Goldman-Rakic**: Working memory and prefrontal cortex
- **Gerald Edelman**: Neural Darwinism; consciousness theory
- **Giulio Tononi**: Integrated information theory of consciousness

## Frontiers

- **Consciousness**: What neural mechanisms give rise to subjective experience? Can we measure it objectively?
- **Brain-Computer Interfaces**: Restoring movement and communication; merging biological and artificial intelligence
- **Neurodegeneration**: Preventing and reversing Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, ALS; no disease-modifying therapies exist for most
- **Connectomics**: Mapping complete neural wiring diagrams; the human brain has ~86 billion neurons and ~100 trillion synapses
- **Artificial vs Biological Intelligence**: What can neuroscience teach AI, and vice versa?

## Applications

- **Neurology**: Stroke treatment, epilepsy management, movement disorders
- **Psychiatry**: Neural basis of mental illness; targeted drug development
- **Neuroprosthetics**: Cochlear implants, retinal prostheses, brain-computer interfaces
- **Education**: Neuroscience-informed learning strategies; critical periods for skill acquisition
- **AI & Robotics**: Neural network architectures inspired by brain circuits
- **Law & Ethics**: Neuroscience of responsibility; brain-based lie detection; cognitive enhancement

## Classic Works

- **"Principles of Neural Science"** by Kandel, Schwartz & Jessell — The definitive neuroscience textbook, comprehensive and authoritative
- **"The Cognitive Neurosciences"** by Gazzaniga — Comprehensive overview of cognitive neuroscience research
- **"Neuroscience: Exploring the Bear"** by Bear, Connors & Paradiso — Accessible introductory textbook widely used in courses
- **"The Tell-Tale Brain"** by V.S. Ramachandran — Engaging exploration of neurological case studies and what they reveal about the mind
