# Genetics

## Overview

Genetics is the branch of biology that studies genes, genetic variation, and heredity in living organisms. It seeks to understand how traits are passed from one generation to the next, how genes function at the molecular level, and how genetic variation contributes to evolution and disease. From Mendel's pea plants to modern CRISPR gene editing, genetics has become central to biology, medicine, agriculture, and biotechnology, offering insights into life's fundamental mechanisms and tools to modify them.

## Key Theories

| Theory | Key Figure | Core Idea |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Laws of Inheritance | Gregor Mendel | Traits inherited as discrete units with dominant/recessive patterns |
| Chromosome Theory | Sutton & Boveri | Genes are located on chromosomes; basis of heredity |
| One Gene-One Enzyme | Beadle & Tatum | Each gene specifies one enzyme; biochemical genetics |
| DNA Double Helix | Watson & Crick | DNA structure explains replication and heredity |
| Neutral Theory | Motoo Kimura | Most molecular evolution is neutral; genetic drift dominates |
| Epigenetic Inheritance | Various | Heritable changes not involving DNA sequence changes |
