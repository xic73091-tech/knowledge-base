# Ecology

## Overview

Ecology is the scientific study of the relationships between living organisms and their environment. It examines how organisms interact with each other and with their physical surroundings, from individual behavior to global ecosystem processes. Ecology spans multiple levels of organization—organisms, populations, communities, ecosystems, landscapes, and the biosphere—and provides essential insights for conservation, resource management, and understanding environmental change in the Anthropocene.
