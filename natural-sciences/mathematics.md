# Mathematics

## Overview

Mathematics is the abstract science of number, quantity, structure, space, and change. It uses rigorous logical reasoning to establish truths about abstract patterns and relationships. Mathematics is the foundational language of all sciences and engineering.

## Core Concepts

### Arithmetic & Number Theory
- **Number Systems**: Natural (ℕ), Integers (ℤ), Rational (ℚ), Real (ℝ), Complex (ℂ)
- **Prime Numbers**: Fundamental theorem of arithmetic; prime distribution
- **Modular Arithmetic**: Clock arithmetic; congruences; applications in cryptography
- **Diophantine Equations**: Integer solutions to polynomial equations

### Algebra
- **Linear Algebra**: Vectors, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, vector spaces
- **Abstract Algebra**: Groups, rings, fields; symmetry and structure
- **Polynomial Algebra**: Factoring, roots, Vieta's formulas
- **Systems of Equations**: Gaussian elimination, matrix methods

### Calculus
- **Differential Calculus**: Limits, derivatives, rates of change, chain rule, product rule
- **Integral Calculus**: Antiderivatives, definite integrals, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- **Multivariable Calculus**: Partial derivatives, multiple integrals, gradient, divergence, curl
- **Differential Equations**: ODEs (ordinary), PDEs (partial); applications in physics and engineering
- **Series & Sequences**: Convergence, Taylor/Maclaurin series, Fourier analysis

### Geometry & Topology
- **Euclidean Geometry**: Points, lines, angles, triangles, circles; axiomatic method
- **Non-Euclidean Geometry**: Hyperbolic, elliptic geometries; parallel postulate
- **Analytic Geometry**: Coordinate systems; conic sections
- **Differential Geometry**: Curves, surfaces, manifolds; curvature
- **Topology**: Properties preserved under continuous deformation; connectedness, compactness

### Probability & Statistics
- **Probability Theory**: Sample spaces, events, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem
- **Random Variables**: Discrete and continuous; distributions (normal, binomial, Poisson)
- **Statistical Inference**: Estimation, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals
- **Regression**: Linear, logistic; correlation vs causation
- **Bayesian Statistics**: Prior, likelihood, posterior; updating beliefs with evidence

### Logic & Foundations
- **Propositional Logic**: AND, OR, NOT, implication, biconditional
- **Predicate Logic**: Quantifiers (∀, ∃), predicates
- **Set Theory**: ZFC axioms; unions, intersections, cardinality
- **Proof Techniques**: Direct, contradiction, contrapositive, induction, construction
- **Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems**: Any sufficiently powerful consistent system is incomplete

### Discrete Mathematics
- **Graph Theory**: Vertices, edges, paths, cycles, trees; Eulerian and Hamiltonian paths
- **Combinatorics**: Permutations, combinations, pigeonhole principle, inclusion-exclusion
- **Number Theory**: Divisibility, GCD, modular arithmetic
- **Boolean Algebra**: Logic gates, digital circuits
- **Cryptography**: RSA, elliptic curve; number theory applications

### Applied Mathematics
- **Numerical Analysis**: Approximation methods, error analysis, numerical integration
- **Optimization**: Linear programming, convex optimization, gradient descent
- **Mathematical Modeling**: Translating real-world problems into mathematical frameworks
- **Game Theory**: Strategic interaction; Nash equilibrium; zero-sum games
- **Chaos Theory**: Sensitive dependence on initial conditions; butterfly effect

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Fundamental Theorem of Calculus | Links differentiation and integration |
| Central Limit Theorem | Sample means approach normal distribution |
| Fundamental Theorem of Algebra | Every non-constant polynomial has a complex root |
| Gödel's Incompleteness | No consistent system can prove all truths about arithmetic |
| Euler's Identity | e^(iπ) + 1 = 0; connects five fundamental constants |

## Important Figures

- **Euclid**: Elements; axiomatic geometry
- **Archimedes**: Calculus precursors; pi approximation
- **Isaac Newton / Gottfried Leibniz**: Independent invention of calculus
- **Leonhard Euler**: Prolific; graph theory, number theory, notation
- **Carl Friedrich Gauss**: Prince of mathematicians; number theory, statistics
- **Georg Cantor**: Set theory; different infinities
- **David Hilbert**: 23 problems; formalism
- **Alan Turing**: Computability theory; Turing machine
- **Emmy Noether**: Abstract algebra; Noether's theorem (symmetry → conservation laws)
- **Srinivasa Ramanujan**: Extraordinary number theory contributions
- **Alexander Grothendieck**: Revolutionized algebraic geometry

## Applications

- **Physics**: Differential equations, group theory, linear algebra
- **Computer Science**: Algorithms, cryptography, discrete math, machine learning
- **Engineering**: Signal processing, control theory, optimization
- **Economics**: Game theory, optimization, stochastic models
- **Medicine**: Statistics, imaging algorithms, epidemiological modeling
- **Finance**: Stochastic calculus, risk modeling, derivatives pricing
