# Complexity Science

## Overview

Complexity science is an interdisciplinary field that studies complex adaptive systems—systems composed of many interacting components that exhibit emergent behavior not easily predicted from the properties of individual components. It provides frameworks for understanding phenomena ranging from ant colonies and ecosystems to economies and cities. Complexity science draws on physics, mathematics, biology, computer science, and social sciences to develop theories and tools for analyzing systems where the whole is fundamentally different from the sum of its parts.
