# Biology

## Overview

Biology is the scientific study of life and living organisms, including their structure, function, growth, origin, evolution, distribution, and taxonomy. It encompasses phenomena from molecular interactions within cells to global ecosystem dynamics.

## Core Concepts

### Cell Biology
- **Cell Theory**: All living things are composed of cells; cells are the basic unit of life
- **Prokaryotes vs Eukaryotes**: No nucleus (bacteria, archaea) vs membrane-bound nucleus
- **Cell Structure**: Nucleus, mitochondria, ribosomes, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi apparatus, lysosomes, cell membrane
- **Cell Division**: Mitosis (growth/repair), meiosis (gametes, genetic diversity)
- **Cell Signaling**: Signal transduction pathways, hormones, neurotransmitters

### Genetics & Molecular Biology
- **DNA Structure**: Double helix, base pairing (A-T, C-G)
- **DNA Replication**: Semi-conservative; helicase, polymerase, ligase
- **Transcription**: DNA → mRNA (in nucleus)
- **Translation**: mRNA → protein (at ribosomes); genetic code, codons
- **Gene Regulation**: Operons, transcription factors, epigenetics
- **Mutations**: Point mutations, insertions, deletions, chromosomal abnormalities
- **CRISPR-Cas9**: Gene editing technology

### Evolution
- **Natural Selection**: Survival of the fittest; differential reproduction
- **Darwin's Theory**: Common descent, variation, selection, gradualism
- **Mechanisms**: Natural selection, genetic drift, gene flow, mutation
- **Speciation**: Allopatric, sympatric; reproductive isolation
- **Evidence**: Fossil record, comparative anatomy, molecular biology, biogeography
- **Modern Synthesis**: Integration of Darwinian evolution with Mendelian genetics

### Ecology
- **Ecosystems**: Biotic and abiotic components; energy flow and nutrient cycling
- **Food Chains & Webs**: Producers, consumers, decomposers; trophic levels
- **Population Ecology**: Growth models (exponential, logistic), carrying capacity
- **Community Ecology**: Succession, competition, predation, symbiosis (mutualism, commensalism, parasitism)
- **Biogeochemical Cycles**: Carbon, nitrogen, water, phosphorus cycles
- **Biodiversity**: Species richness, ecosystem services

### Physiology
- **Homeostasis**: Maintaining internal stability (temperature, pH, blood sugar)
- **Nervous System**: Neurons, synapses, action potentials, brain structure
- **Endocrine System**: Hormones, feedback loops
- **Circulatory System**: Heart, blood vessels, blood components
- **Respiratory System**: Gas exchange, lungs
- **Digestive System**: Nutrient absorption, enzymes
- **Immune System**: Innate vs adaptive immunity, antibodies, T-cells, B-cells

### Biochemistry
- **Macromolecules**: Proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, nucleic acids
- **Enzymes**: Catalysts, substrate specificity, Michaelis-Menten kinetics
- **Metabolism**: Catabolism (breakdown) and anabolism (building)
- **Cellular Respiration**: Glycolysis → Krebs cycle → oxidative phosphorylation (ATP)
- **Photosynthesis**: Light reactions, Calvin cycle; CO₂ + H₂O → glucose + O₂

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Cell Theory | All life composed of cells |
| Evolution by Natural Selection | Species change over time via selection pressures |
| Gene Theory | Traits inherited via discrete units (genes) |
| Central Dogma | DNA → RNA → protein (with exceptions) |
| Endosymbiosis | Mitochondria and chloroplasts originated from engulfed prokaryotes |

## Important Figures

- **Charles Darwin**: Theory of evolution by natural selection
- **Gregor Mendel**: Father of genetics; inheritance laws
- **Rosalind Franklin**: X-ray crystallography of DNA
- **Watson & Crick**: DNA double helix structure
- **Louis Pasteur**: Germ theory, pasteurization
- **Alexander Fleming**: Discovered penicillin
- **Barbara McClintock**: Transposable elements ("jumping genes")

## Frontiers

- **Protein Folding Problem**: Predicting 3D protein structure from amino acid sequence; major progress with AlphaFold but many challenges remain
- **Consciousness and Neural Correlates**: How subjective experience arises from neural activity; one of the deepest unsolved questions in biology
- **Horizontal Gene Transfer**: The extent and impact of gene transfer between unrelated organisms, especially in prokaryotes and its role in evolution
- **Microbiome-Host Interactions**: Understanding how trillions of commensal organisms influence health, immunity, and behavior
- **Synthetic Minimal Cells**: Building the simplest possible living cell from scratch to understand the boundary between chemistry and life

## Applications

- **Medicine**: Disease understanding, drug development, gene therapy
- **Agriculture**: Crop breeding, GMOs, pest management
- **Biotechnology**: Recombinant DNA, synthetic biology, biofuels
- **Conservation**: Biodiversity preservation, ecosystem restoration
- **Forensics**: DNA profiling, forensic biology

## Classic Works

- **"Molecular Biology of the Gene"** by James Watson et al. — Definitive molecular biology textbook covering DNA, RNA, and gene expression in depth
- **"Campbell Biology"** by Urry, Cain et al. — The standard introductory biology text; comprehensive coverage of all major topics with clear illustrations
- **"The Selfish Gene"** by Richard Dawkins — Popular science classic that reframed evolution from the gene's perspective; profoundly influential
- **"On the Origin of Species"** by Charles Darwin — The foundational text of evolutionary biology; still essential reading for understanding natural selection
- **"Molecular Biology of the Cell"** by Alberts et al. — Authoritative cell biology textbook; the go-to reference for cellular structure and function
