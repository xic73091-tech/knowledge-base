# Nursing

## Overview

Nursing is a healthcare profession focused on the care of individuals, families, and communities to attain, maintain, or recover optimal health and quality of life. Nurses work across all healthcare settings and combine clinical expertise with compassionate care, health education, and advocacy.

## Core Concepts

### Nursing Theory & Frameworks
- **Nightingale's Environmental Theory**: Clean environment promotes healing; sanitation, ventilation, light
- **Orem's Self-Care Deficit**: Nursing needed when patient cannot meet self-care needs
- **Roy's Adaptation Model**: Patient adapts to stimuli; nursing promotes adaptation
- **Watson's Human Caring**: Caring as central to nursing; transpersonal caring
- **Maslow's Hierarchy**: Prioritize needs — physiological, safety, love/belonging, esteem, self-actualization
- **Nursing Process**: Assessment → Diagnosis → Planning → Implementation → Evaluation (ADPIE)

### Clinical Skills
- **Vital Signs**: Temperature, pulse, blood pressure, respiratory rate, SpO2, pain assessment
- **Physical Assessment**: Head-to-toe assessment; focused assessments by system
- **Medication Administration**: Rights (patient, drug, dose, route, time, documentation); IV administration
- **Wound Care**: Assessment, cleaning, dressing, debridement, negative pressure therapy
- **Infection Control**: Hand hygiene, PPE, sterile technique, isolation precautions, asepsis
- **IV Therapy**: Peripheral and central lines; fluid management; TPN
- **Catheterization**: Urinary catheters; care and maintenance; CAUTI prevention

### Nursing Specialties
- **Medical-Surgical Nursing**: Adult patients with acute and chronic conditions
- **Critical Care (ICU)**: Ventilator management, hemodynamic monitoring, sedation, vasopressors
- **Emergency Nursing**: Trauma care, triage, rapid assessment, stabilization
- **Pediatric Nursing**: Child health, developmental stages, family-centered care
- **Obstetric Nursing**: Labor and delivery, postpartum care, neonatal care
- **Psychiatric Nursing**: Mental health assessment, therapeutic communication, crisis intervention
- **Community Health Nursing**: Home health, school nursing, occupational health, public health
- **Oncology Nursing**: Chemotherapy administration, symptom management, palliative care
- **Perioperative Nursing**: Pre-op, intra-op (scrub, circulating), post-op (PACU) care

### Patient Safety
- **Medication Safety**: Barcode scanning, double-checks, high-alert medications
- **Fall Prevention**: Risk assessment (Morse Fall Scale), environmental modifications, bed alarms
- **Pressure Injury Prevention**: Braden scale, repositioning, pressure-relieving surfaces
- **Patient Identification**: Two-identifier policy; wristband verification
- **Handoff Communication**: SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation)
- **Error Reporting**: Just culture; near-miss reporting; root cause analysis

### Evidence-Based Practice (EBP)
- **Research Process**: Clinical questions (PICOT), literature review, critical appraisal
- **Levels of Evidence**: Systematic reviews > RCTs > cohort > case-control > case series > expert opinion
- **Quality Improvement**: PDSA cycle; Six Sigma; Lean methodology
- **Clinical Practice Guidelines**: Evidence-based recommendations; guideline implementation

### Nursing Ethics
- **Code of Ethics**: ANA Code; beneficence, nonmaleficence, autonomy, justice, fidelity
- **Informed Consent**: Patient understanding of procedures, risks, alternatives
- **Confidentiality**: HIPAA; patient privacy; breach reporting
- **End-of-Life Care**: Advance directives, palliative care, hospice, do-not-resuscitate
- **Advocacy**: Speaking up for patients; navigating healthcare systems; ethical dilemmas

### Health Education & Promotion
- **Patient Education**: Teach-back method; health literacy; culturally competent education
- **Discharge Planning**: Transition of care; medication reconciliation; follow-up
- **Chronic Disease Management**: Self-management support; lifestyle modification
- **Community Outreach**: Health screenings, immunization drives, health fairs

- **Betty Neuer**: Systems model; goal-oriented nursing

## Frontiers

- **AI and Clinical Decision Support**: How will predictive algorithms for sepsis, falls, and deterioration integrate into nursing workflows without increasing alert fatigue or eroding clinical judgment?
- **Nursing Workforce Crisis**: Burnout, moral injury, and mass exits post-pandemic — evidence-based strategies for retention, safe staffing ratios, and sustainable work design are actively debated
- **Telehealth and Remote Monitoring**: Expanding nursing roles in virtual care, remote patient monitoring, and hospital-at-home models requires new competency frameworks and reimbursement structures
- **Social Determinants in Nursing Practice**: Systematically screening for and addressing housing, food, and transportation barriers at the bedside — implementation science is still catching up
- **Precision Nursing**: Applying genomics and biomarker data to individualize nursing interventions (e.g., pain management, wound healing) mirrors precision medicine but lacks a robust evidence base

## Applications

- **Hospital Nursing**: Bedside care, medication administration, patient monitoring
- **Community Health**: Home health visits, school nursing, public health clinics
- **Advanced Practice**: Nurse practitioners, clinical nurse specialists, nurse anesthetists, nurse midwives
- **Nursing Education**: Teaching, simulation, clinical supervision
- **Nursing Informatics**: EHR systems, clinical decision support, data analytics
- **Research**: Clinical trials, outcomes research, nursing science

## Classic Works

- **"Fundamentals of Nursing"** by Patricia Potter and Anne Perry — Comprehensive textbook covering core nursing concepts, clinical skills, and the nursing process; a standard in nursing education
- **"Brunner & Suddarth's Textbook of Medical-Surgical Nursing"** by Janice Hinkle and Kerry Cheever — The go-to reference for adult medical-surgical nursing across all body systems
- **"Nursing: The Philosophy and Science of Caring"** by Jean Watson — Foundational text on human caring theory; redefined nursing as a moral and philosophical practice
- **"Notes on Nursing: What It Is, and What It Is Not"** by Florence Nightingale — The 1860 work that launched modern nursing; remarkably relevant observations on environment and patient care
- **"Nurse's Pocket Guide: Diagnoses, Prioritized Interventions, and Rationales"** by Marilynn Doenges et al. — Practical clinical reference linking nursing diagnoses to evidence-based interventions
