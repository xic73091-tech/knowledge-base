# Nutrition

## Overview

Nutrition is the science that interprets the interaction of nutrients and other substances in food in relation to maintenance, growth, reproduction, health, and disease of an organism. It includes food intake, absorption, assimilation, biosynthesis, catabolism, and excretion.

## Core Concepts

### Macronutrients
- **Carbohydrates**: Simple (sugars) and complex (starches, fiber); glycemic index; energy source (4 kcal/g)
- **Proteins**: Amino acids (essential vs non-essential); complete vs incomplete proteins; functions: structure, enzymes, immunity (4 kcal/g)
- **Fats/Lipids**: Saturated, unsaturated (mono/poly), trans fats; essential fatty acids (omega-3, omega-6); energy storage (9 kcal/g)
- **Fiber**: Soluble (oats, beans — lowers cholesterol) and insoluble (whole grains — promotes bowel health)
- **Water**: Essential for all bodily functions; hydration needs; electrolyte balance

### Micronutrients
- **Vitamins**:
  - Fat-soluble: A (vision), D (bone health, immunity), E (antioxidant), K (blood clotting)
  - Water-soluble: C (antioxidant, collagen), B-complex (energy metabolism, nerve function)
- **Minerals**: Calcium (bones), iron (oxygen transport), zinc (immunity), magnesium (muscle/nerve), potassium (blood pressure), sodium (fluid balance), iodine (thyroid)
- **Trace Elements**: Selenium, copper, manganese, chromium, fluoride

### Energy Balance
- **Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)**: Energy at rest; Harris-Benedict equation; affected by age, sex, body composition
- **Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)**: BMR + activity + thermic effect of food
- **Caloric Balance**: Surplus → weight gain; deficit → weight loss; maintenance → stable weight
- **Body Composition**: BMI limitations; body fat percentage; waist circumference; DEXA scan

### Dietary Patterns
- **Mediterranean Diet**: Olive oil, fish, vegetables, whole grains, moderate wine; cardiovascular benefits
- **DASH Diet**: Reduces hypertension; high in fruits, vegetables, low-fat dairy; low sodium
- **Plant-Based Diets**: Vegetarian, vegan; health benefits; potential nutrient concerns (B12, iron, omega-3)
- **Ketogenic Diet**: Very low carb, high fat; ketosis; weight loss; epilepsy management
- **Intermittent Fasting**: Time-restricted eating; 16:8, 5:2; autophagy; metabolic benefits
- **Western Diet**: High in processed foods, sugar, saturated fat; associated with chronic disease

### Nutrition & Disease
- **Obesity**: Causes, consequences (diabetes, cardiovascular disease, cancer), management
- **Diabetes & Nutrition**: Glycemic control; carbohydrate counting; meal planning
- **Cardiovascular Disease**: Saturated fat, trans fat, sodium, fiber, omega-3 roles
- **Cancer**: Dietary risk factors (processed meat, alcohol); protective factors (fiber, antioxidants)
- **Osteoporosis**: Calcium, vitamin D, protein; weight-bearing exercise
- **Malnutrition**: Undernutrition, overnutrition, micronutrient deficiencies; global burden

### Food Science & Safety
- **Food Processing**: Minimal to ultra-processed; nutrient retention; additives
- **Food Additives**: Preservatives, colorings, flavor enhancers; safety regulation (FDA, EFSA)
- **Food Contamination**: Biological (bacteria, viruses), chemical (pesticides, heavy metals), physical
- **Food Labeling**: Nutrition facts; serving sizes; health claims; ingredient lists
- **Organic & GMO**: Organic certification; genetically modified organisms; safety debate

### Sports Nutrition
- **Macronutrient Timing**: Pre-, intra-, post-workout nutrition
- **Protein for Athletes**: 1.2-2.0 g/kg/day; leucine threshold; protein synthesis
- **Hydration**: Sweat loss; electrolyte replacement; hyponatremia risk
- **Ergogenic Aids**: Caffeine, creatine, beta-alanine, nitrate; evidence-based vs marketing
- **Endurance vs Strength**: Different nutritional needs; glycogen loading; carb periodization

### Life Stage Nutrition
- **Pregnancy**: Folic acid, iron, calcium, DHA; weight gain guidelines; food safety
- **Infants & Children**: Breastfeeding; complementary feeding; growth monitoring; childhood obesity
- **Adolescents**: Growth spurts; iron needs; eating disorders
- **Older Adults**: Protein needs; vitamin D, B12, calcium; sarcopenia; hydration

## Important Figures

- **Casimir Funk**: Vitamins; coined term "vitamine"
- **Ancel Keys**: Seven Countries Study; Mediterranean diet research
- **Wilbur Atwater**: Calorimetry; nutrition science pioneer
- **T. Colin Campbell**: The China Study; plant-based nutrition research

## Frontiers

- **Personalized Nutrition**: Can microbiome composition, glycemic response patterns, and genetic variants predict individual dietary responses better than population-level guidelines? Large trials (e.g., PREDICT) are underway
- **Ultra-Processed Food and Health**: Mounting epidemiologic evidence links UPF consumption to chronic disease, but the causal mechanisms — additives, nutrient displacement, processing itself — remain unclear
- **Nutrition and Mental Health**: The gut-brain axis and dietary patterns (e.g., Mediterranean diet) show promise for depression and anxiety prevention, yet clinical trial evidence is still limited
- **Sustainable Diets**: Balancing human nutritional needs with planetary boundaries (carbon, water, land use) requires rethinking global food systems — EAT-Lancet benchmarks are debated
- **Chrononutrition**: How meal timing, circadian rhythms, and fasting windows interact with metabolism and disease risk is an emerging field with few long-term randomized trials

## Applications

- **Clinical Nutrition**: Medical nutrition therapy; tube feeding; TPN
- **Dietetics**: Registered dietitians; nutrition counseling; meal planning
- **Public Health**: Nutrition policy; food fortification; school lunch programs
- **Food Industry**: Product development; reformulation; labeling compliance
- **Sports Nutrition**: Athletic performance; body composition; recovery
- **Global Health**: Malnutrition programs; micronutrient supplementation; food security

## Classic Works

- **"Present Knowledge in Nutrition"** by John Erdman Jr., Ian Macdonald, and Steven Zeisel — Authoritative reference covering every nutrient, metabolism, and clinical application; the standard in nutrition science
- **"Williams' Basic Nutrition and Diet Therapy"** by Staci Nix — Accessible introduction to nutrition principles; widely used in dietetics and nursing programs
- **"The China Study"** by T. Colin Campbell and Thomas Campbell — Influential (if debated) large-scale study linking plant-based diets to lower chronic disease rates
- **"How Not to Die"** by Michael Greger — Evidence-based guide to dietary prevention of leading causes of death; accessible to general readers
- **"Food and Nutrition"** by Barbara Bowman and Robert Russell — Comprehensive reference on global nutrition challenges, policy, and science
