# Mental Health

## Overview

Mental health encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how people think, feel, act, make decisions, and relate to others. Mental disorders are conditions that affect mood, thinking, and behavior, causing significant distress and functional impairment. They are common, treatable, and deserving of the same attention as physical health.

## Core Concepts

### Common Mental Disorders
- **Depression**: Persistent sadness, loss of interest, fatigue, sleep/appetite changes, suicidal ideation; MDD, persistent depressive disorder, bipolar depression
- **Anxiety Disorders**: Generalized anxiety (GAD), social anxiety, specific phobias, panic disorder; excessive worry, avoidance, physical symptoms
- **Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)**: Intrusive obsessions, compulsive rituals; types: contamination, checking, symmetry, harm
- **Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)**: Flashbacks, nightmares, hypervigilance, avoidance after trauma; complex PTSD
- **Bipolar Disorder**: Manic episodes (euphoria, grandiosity, decreased need for sleep) alternating with depression
- **Schizophrenia**: Positive symptoms (hallucinations, delusions), negative symptoms (flat affect, social withdrawal), cognitive symptoms
- **Eating Disorders**: Anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, binge eating disorder; body image disturbance
- **Personality Disorders**: Borderline (emotional instability, identity disturbance), antisocial, narcissistic, avoidant, obsessive-compulsive PD
- **Substance Use Disorders**: Alcohol, opioids, stimulants, cannabis; tolerance, withdrawal, craving, impairment
- **ADHD**: Inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity; childhood-onset; persists into adulthood
- **Autism Spectrum Disorder**: Social communication difficulties, restricted/repetitive behaviors; spectrum of severity

### Therapeutic Approaches
- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)**: Identify and change maladaptive thoughts and behaviors; evidence-based for depression, anxiety, PTSD, OCD
- **Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)**: For borderline PD and chronic suicidality; mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, interpersonal effectiveness
- **Psychodynamic Therapy**: Unconscious processes, early experiences, transference; insight-oriented
- **Humanistic/Person-Centered Therapy**: Unconditional positive regard, empathy, genuineness (Rogers)
- **EMDR**: Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing; for trauma processing
- **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)**: Acceptance, mindfulness, values-based action
- **Exposure Therapy**: Gradual exposure to feared stimuli; for phobias, PTSD, OCD
- **Family/Couples Therapy**: Systemic approach; communication patterns; attachment

### Psychopharmacology
- **Antidepressants**: SSRIs (fluoxetine, sertraline), SNRIs (venlafaxine), TCAs, MAOIs; mechanism: serotonin/norepinephrine reuptake inhibition
- **Anxiolytics**: Benzodiazepines (short-term), buspirone, SSRIs (first-line for GAD)
- **Mood Stabilizers**: Lithium, valproate, lamotrigine; for bipolar disorder
- **Antipsychotics**: Typical (haloperidol) and atypical (clozapine, risperidone, olanzapine); dopamine/serotonin antagonism
- **Stimulants**: Methylphenidate, amphetamines; for ADHD; dopamine/norepinephrine enhancement
- **Medication Management**: Dosing, side effects, monitoring, adherence, discontinuation

### Suicide Prevention
- **Risk Factors**: Prior attempts, depression, substance use, hopelessness, social isolation, access to means, recent loss
- **Protective Factors**: Social support, reasons for living, treatment engagement, coping skills
- **Assessment**: Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS); direct questioning
- **Interventions**: Safety planning, means restriction, crisis hotlines, follow-up care, hospitalization when needed
- **Postvention**: Support after suicide loss; grief counseling; community response

### Mental Health Systems
- **Community Mental Health**: Outpatient clinics, crisis services, case management, supported housing
- **Inpatient Psychiatric Care**: Acute stabilization; involuntary commitment criteria
- **Recovery Model**: Hope, empowerment, meaning, connection; person-centered; lived experience
- **Stepped Care**: Start with least intensive intervention; escalate as needed
- **Integrated Care**: Mental health in primary care; collaborative care models

### Stigma & Advocacy
- **Public Stigma**: Negative attitudes, discrimination, social distance
- **Self-Stigma**: Internalized shame, reduced help-seeking
- **Anti-Stigma Programs**: Contact-based education, media campaigns, mental health literacy
- **Advocacy**: Policy reform, funding parity, insurance coverage, disability rights

### Positive Psychology & Well-Being
- **PERMA Model** (Seligman): Positive emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Accomplishment
- **Resilience**: Ability to recover from adversity; protective factors; growth after trauma
- **Mindfulness**: Present-moment awareness; MBSR, MBCT; evidence for depression prevention
- **Gratitude & Strengths**: Character strengths (VIA); gratitude journaling; evidence-based positive interventions

### Child & Adolescent Mental Health
- **Developmental Disorders**: Autism, ADHD, intellectual disability, specific learning disorders
- **Childhood Anxiety & Depression**: Different presentations than adults; school-based interventions
- **Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs)**: Abuse, neglect, household dysfunction; long-term health impacts
- **School Mental Health**: Prevention programs, counseling, threat assessment, social-emotional learning

## Frontiers

- **Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy**: Psilocybin, MDMA, and ketamine show rapid and sustained effects for depression, PTSD, and addiction, but optimal protocols, long-term safety, and mechanisms of action are still being defined
- **Digital Phenotyping**: Passive smartphone and wearable data (sleep, movement, social interaction) may enable early detection of mood episodes and psychotic relapse — privacy and clinical validity are unresolved
- **Biomarkers for Mental Illness**: Blood-based markers, neuroimaging signatures, and genetic risk scores could move psychiatry toward objective diagnosis, but replication and clinical utility remain elusive
- **Transdiagnostic Approaches**: Treating shared mechanisms (e.g., emotion dysregulation, negative valence) across diagnostic categories rather than discrete disorders — reshaping both research and treatment frameworks
- **Global Mental Health Scale-Up**: Delivering evidence-based interventions in low-resource settings through task-shifting, digital platforms, and community-based models is a massive implementation challenge

## Applications

- **Clinical Practice**: Assessment, diagnosis, therapy, medication management
- **Crisis Intervention**: Hotlines, mobile crisis teams, emergency departments
- **Workplace Mental Health**: Employee assistance programs, burnout prevention, psychological safety
- **School Psychology**: Counseling, assessment, behavioral intervention
- **Digital Mental Health**: Teletherapy, mental health apps, AI-assisted screening
- **Forensic Mental Health**: Competency evaluations, criminal responsibility, risk assessment

## Classic Works

- **"Cognitive Therapy of Depression"** by Aaron Beck et al. — The foundational text on cognitive therapy; articulates the cognitive model and its application to depression with empirical rigor
- **"Man's Search for Meaning"** by Viktor Frankl — Memoir and theoretical statement on logotherapy; demonstrates how meaning-making sustains psychological survival under extreme suffering
- **"Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-5-TR)"** by the American Psychiatric Association — The standard classification system for mental disorders in clinical and research settings
- **"Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment of Borderline Personality Disorder"** by Marsha Linehan — The manual that operationalized DBT; transformed treatment of a previously "untreatable" population
- **"An Unquiet Mind"** by Kay Redfield Jamison — A psychiatrist's memoir of living with bipolar disorder; broke ground in reducing stigma through personal disclosure
