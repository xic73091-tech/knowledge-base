# Surgery

## Overview

Surgery is a medical specialty that uses operative manual and instrumental techniques to investigate or treat a pathological condition such as a disease or injury, to help improve bodily function or appearance, or to repair unwanted ruptured areas. Surgery spans numerous subspecialties organized by body system, patient age, or technique. Modern surgery has evolved from open procedures to minimally invasive and robotic approaches, dramatically improving patient outcomes, reducing recovery times, and expanding the range of treatable conditions.
