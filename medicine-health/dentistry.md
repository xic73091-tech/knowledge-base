# Dentistry & Oral Health

## Overview

Dentistry is the branch of medicine focused on the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of conditions affecting the oral cavity, maxillofacial region, and adjacent structures. It encompasses everything from routine preventive care to complex surgical reconstruction. Oral health is increasingly recognized as integral to overall systemic health.

## Core Concepts

### Dental Anatomy & Histology
- **Tooth Structure**: Enamel (hardest tissue), dentin, pulp (nerves, blood vessels), cementum, periodontal ligament
- **Tooth Types**: Incisors (cutting), canines (tearing), premolars (crushing), molars (grinding)
- **Occlusion**: How upper and lower teeth meet; malocclusion types and consequences
- **Jaw & TMJ**: Temporomandibular joint; mastication muscles; TMJ disorders

### Preventive Dentistry
- **Oral Hygiene**: Brushing, flossing, interdental cleaning; plaque and calculus control
- **Fluoride**: Systemic and topical; mechanism of caries prevention; water fluoridation
- **Sealants**: Protective coatings on molars; caries prevention in children
- **Diet & Oral Health**: Sugar, acid, and caries risk; erosion from acidic foods and drinks
- **Screening**: Oral cancer screening; periodontal assessment; caries risk assessment

### Dental Caries & Restorative Dentistry
- **Caries Etiology**: Bacterial biofilm (Streptococcus mutans), fermentable carbohydrates, susceptible host, time
- **Caries Detection**: Visual-tactile, radiographs, laser fluorescence (DIAGNOdent), ICDAS classification
- **Restorative Materials**: Amalgam, composite resin, glass ionomer, ceramic, gold
- **Direct Restorations**: Fillings (Class I-V); bonding agents; layering techniques
- **Indirect Restorations**: Inlays, onlays, crowns; CAD/CAM technology

### Periodontics
- **Gingivitis**: Inflammation of gingiva; reversible with oral hygiene improvement
- **Periodontitis**: Progressive attachment and bone loss; pockets, recession, tooth mobility
- **Risk Factors**: Smoking, diabetes, genetics, immunosuppression, stress
- **Treatment**: Scaling and root planing; surgical (flap surgery, bone grafts); laser therapy
- **Implant-Related**: Peri-implantitis; maintenance of dental implants

### Endodontics
- **Pulp Diseases**: Pulpitis (reversible, irreversible); pulp necrosis
- **Root Canal Therapy**: Access, cleaning, shaping, obturation; rotary instrumentation
- **Apicoectomy**: Surgical root-end resection for persistent infections
- **Regenerative Endodontics**: Revascularization; stem cell approaches for immature teeth

### Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery
- **Tooth Extraction**: Simple and surgical extractions; third molars (wisdom teeth)
- **Dental Implants**: Osseointegration; implant placement; bone grafting for deficient ridges
- **Orthognathic Surgery**: Corrective jaw surgery for skeletal malocclusions
- **Trauma**: Facial fractures; soft tissue injuries; avulsed teeth reimplantation
- **Pathology**: Cysts, tumors; oral cancer staging and treatment

### Orthodontics
- **Malocclusion Classification**: Angle Class I, II, III; crowding, spacing, crossbite
- **Fixed Appliances**: Brackets, wires, elastics; biomechanics of tooth movement
- **Clear Aligners**: Invisalign-type systems; digital treatment planning
- **Growth Modification**: Functional appliances; headgear; palatal expansion
- **Retention**: Preventing relapse; fixed vs removable retainers

### Prosthodontics
- **Fixed Prostheses**: Bridges, crowns; materials (PFM, zirconia, lithium disilicate)
- **Removable Prostheses**: Complete and partial dentures; implant-supported overdentures
- **Maxillofacial Prosthetics**: Obturators, orbital prostheses; rehabilitation after cancer surgery
- **Digital Dentistry**: Intraoral scanning, 3D printing, CAD/CAM milling

### Pediatric Dentistry
- **Primary Teeth**: Development, eruption patterns, importance for space maintenance
- **Behavior Management**: Tell-show-do, sedation, general anesthesia
- **Early Childhood Caries**: Prevention, risk factors, treatment
- **Developmental Anomalies**: Amelogenesis imperfecta, dentinogenesis imperfecta, ectodermal dysplasia

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Oral-Systemic Link | Periodontal disease associated with cardiovascular disease, diabetes, adverse pregnancy outcomes |
| Ecological Plaque Hypothesis | Changes in oral environment shift microbial balance toward disease |
| Osseointegration | Direct structural bone-to-implant contact; foundation of dental implants |
| Wolff's Law | Bone remodels in response to mechanical forces; basis for orthodontic tooth movement |

## Important Figures

- **Pierre Fauchard**: Father of modern dentistry; Le Chirurgien Dentiste (1728)
- **G.V. Black**: Father of operative dentistry; cavity classification system
- **Per-Ingvar Brånemark**: Discovered osseointegration; pioneered dental implants
- **Edward Angle**: Father of modern orthodontics; malocclusion classification
- **Willoughby Miller**: Linked oral bacteria to dental caries

## Frontiers

- **Regenerative Dentistry**: Stem cell-based tooth regeneration; bioengineered dental tissues; can we grow new teeth?
- **AI in Dentistry**: Automated caries detection, treatment planning, orthodontic prediction from imaging data
- **Saliva Diagnostics**: Using saliva as a non-invasive diagnostic fluid for systemic diseases, cancer biomarkers, and drug detection
- **3D Bioprinting**: Printing dental tissues, periodontal structures, and eventually whole teeth
- **Minimally Invasive Dentistry**: Resin infiltration, remineralization therapies; shifting from surgical to biological approaches

## Applications

- **General Practice**: Preventive care, restorative treatment, patient education
- **Specialist Care**: Orthodontics, periodontics, endodontics, oral surgery, prosthodontics
- **Public Health**: Community water fluoridation, school dental programs, access to care
- **Forensic Dentistry**: Identification from dental records; bite mark analysis
- **Cosmetic Dentistry**: Whitening, veneers, smile design
- **Geriatric Dentistry**: Managing oral health in aging populations; dry root caries; denture care

## Classic Works

- **"Sturdevant's Art and Science of Operative Dentistry"** by Roberson et al. — The definitive textbook on restorative dentistry techniques and materials
- **"Carranza's Clinical Periodontology"** by Newman et al. — Comprehensive reference for periodontal disease and treatment
- **"Contemporary Orthodontics"** by Proffit et al. — Widely used textbook covering orthodontic diagnosis and treatment mechanics
- **"Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery"** by Fonseca et al. — Authoritative multi-volume reference for surgical procedures
- **"Pathways of the Pulp"** by Cohen & Hargreaves — The gold standard endodontics textbook
