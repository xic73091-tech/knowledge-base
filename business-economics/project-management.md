# Project Management

## Overview

Project management is the application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to project activities to meet project requirements. It involves planning, organizing, and managing resources to bring about the successful completion of specific project goals and objectives. Project managers must balance competing demands for scope, time, cost, quality, resources, and risk. Effective project management is critical across all industries and organizational functions, enabling teams to deliver results efficiently and effectively.

## Core Concepts

### Project Management Fundamentals
- **Project Definition**: Temporary endeavor; unique product/service; specific objectives
- **Programs vs Portfolios**: Group of related projects; coordinated management for strategic objectives
- **Project Lifecycle**: Initiating; planning; executing; monitoring/controlling; closing
- **Project Stakeholders**: Individuals/groups affected by or can affect the project
- **Project Constraints**: Scope; schedule; budget; quality; resources; risk
- **Triple Constraint**: Balancing time; cost; and scope; quality as integration point
- **Project Success Criteria**: On-time; on-budget; meeting requirements; stakeholder satisfaction

### Project Integration Management
- **Project Charter**: Authorization document; defines project; authorizes manager
- **Project Management Plan**: Baseline documents; integration of all planning outputs
- **Direct & Manage Project Work**: Leading and performing project work
- **Monitor & Control Project Work**: Tracking; reviewing; reporting progress
- **Integrated Change Control**: Reviewing; approving; managing changes
- **Close Project or Phase**: Finalizing all activities; transitioning deliverables
- **Lessons Learned**: Documenting knowledge for future projects

### Project Scope Management
- **Plan Scope Management**: Creating scope management plan
- **Collect Requirements**: Determining stakeholder needs; documenting requirements
- **Define Scope**: Developing detailed project scope statement
- **Create WBS**: Work Breakdown Structure; decomposing work into manageable components
- **Validate Scope**: Formal acceptance of deliverables
- **Control Scope**: Monitoring project status; managing scope changes
- **Scope Creep**: Uncontrolled expansion; change management; scope baseline protection

### Project Schedule Management
- **Plan Schedule Management**: Establishing policies for planning schedule
- **Define Activities**: Identifying actions to produce deliverables
- **Sequence Activities**: Identifying dependencies; activity relationships
- **Estimate Activity Durations**: Resource estimates; duration estimates
- **Develop Schedule**: Analyzing sequences; durations; constraints
- **Control Schedule**: Monitoring progress; schedule baseline management
- **Critical Path Method (CPM)**: Longest path; schedule flexibility; critical activities

- **Plan Cost Management**: Establishing cost management procedures

- **Control Costs**: Monitoring cost performance; variance management
- **Earned Value Management (EVM)**: Performance measurement; CPI; SPI; forecasting
- **Cost Baseline**: Time-phased budget for measuring performance

### Project Quality Management
- **Plan Quality Management**: Identifying quality requirements; standards for project

- **Control Quality**: Monitoring results; ensuring deliverables meet requirements
- **Quality Planning**: Tools like cost-benefit analysis; benchmarking
- **Quality Assurance**: Process improvement; audit; prevention over inspection

- **Monitor Communications**: Ensuring information flow effectiveness
- **Communication Technologies**: Email; instant messaging; video conferencing; collaboration tools
- **Communication Models**: Sender-receiver; encoding/decoding; noise; feedback

### Project Risk Response Strategies
- **Avoid**: Eliminating risk threat; changing approach
- **Transfer**: Shifting impact to third party; insurance; contracts
- **Mitigate**: Reducing probability/impact; proactive measures
- **Accept**: Acknowledging risk; contingency plans; watch list
- **Escalate**: Informing appropriate level; not managing at current level
- **Contingency Reserves**: Time/budget for known unknowns
- **Management Reserves**: Time/budget for unknown unknowns

### Earned Value Management (EVM)
- **Planned Value (PV)**: Budgeted cost of scheduled work
- **Earned Value (EV)**: Budgeted cost of completed work
- **Actual Cost (AC)**: Actual cost of completed work
- **Cost Variance (CV)**: EV minus AC; under/over budget
- **Schedule Variance (SV)**: EV minus PV; ahead/behind schedule
- **Cost Performance Index (CPI)**: EV/AC; efficiency of cost spending
- **Schedule Performance Index (SPI)**: EV/PV; efficiency of time usage
- **Estimate at Completion (EAC)**: Projected total cost; forecasting methods
- **Estimate to Complete (ETC)**: Expected cost to finish remaining work
- **Variance at Completion (VAC)**: Difference between BAC and EAC

- **Transition to Operations**: Handover to steady-state; support arrangements
