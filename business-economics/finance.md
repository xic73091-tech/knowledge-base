# Finance

## Overview

Finance is the study and management of money, investments, and financial instruments. It encompasses personal finance, corporate finance, and public finance, as well as financial markets and institutions. Finance applies economic principles, mathematics, and accounting to evaluate risk, return, and value.

## Core Concepts

### Financial Markets
- **Stock Markets**: Equity ownership; exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ, Shanghai, London); IPOs; market capitalization
- **Bond Markets**: Debt instruments; government, corporate, municipal; yield, coupon, maturity; credit ratings
- **Money Markets**: Short-term instruments; T-bills, commercial paper, certificates of deposit
- **Foreign Exchange (Forex)**: Currency trading; exchange rates; spot and forward markets
- **Commodity Markets**: Oil, gold, agricultural products; futures contracts
- **Derivatives Markets**: Options, futures, swaps, forwards; hedging and speculation

### Valuation & Analysis
- **Time Value of Money**: Present value (PV), future value (FV), discounting, compounding; NPV, IRR
- **Financial Ratios**: P/E ratio, P/B, EV/EBITDA, ROE, ROA, current ratio, debt-to-equity
- **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)**: Intrinsic value from projected cash flows; WACC as discount rate
- **Comparable Analysis**: Valuation using multiples from similar companies
- **Technical Analysis**: Chart patterns, moving averages, RSI, MACD; price and volume trends
- **Fundamental Analysis**: Financial statements, industry analysis, competitive advantage, management quality

### Corporate Finance
- **Capital Budgeting**: Investment decisions; NPV, IRR, payback period, profitability index
- **Capital Structure**: Debt vs equity financing; weighted average cost of capital (WACC); Modigliani-Miller theorem
- **Working Capital Management**: Cash, inventory, receivables, payables management
- **Dividend Policy**: Dividend payout, share buybacks; dividend irrelevance (Miller-Modigliani)
- **Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A)**: Types (horizontal, vertical, conglomerate); synergy; due diligence; LBO
- **Financial Planning**: Budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis

### Investments
- **Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT)**: Markowitz; efficient frontier; diversification reduces risk
- **Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)**: Expected return = risk-free rate + β(market return - risk-free rate)
- **Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH)**: Weak, semi-strong, strong forms; prices reflect all available information
- **Asset Allocation**: Stocks, bonds, real estate, alternatives; strategic vs tactical
- **Active vs Passive Management**: Index funds; alpha generation; expense ratios
- **Alternative Investments**: Private equity, hedge funds, real estate, commodities, venture capital

### Risk Management
- **Risk Types**: Market risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, operational risk, systemic risk
- **Hedging**: Using derivatives to reduce exposure; options, futures, swaps
- **Value at Risk (VaR)**: Maximum expected loss over a given time period at a given confidence level
- **Insurance**: Risk transfer; actuarial science; underwriting
- **Stress Testing**: Scenario analysis; extreme event modeling

### Personal Finance
- **Budgeting**: Income, expenses, savings rate; 50/30/20 rule
- **Emergency Fund**: 3-6 months expenses; liquidity
- **Retirement Planning**: 401(k), IRA, pension; compound interest; target-date funds
- **Debt Management**: Good debt vs bad debt; avalanche vs snowball methods; credit scores
- **Insurance**: Life, health, disability, property, liability
- **Estate Planning**: Wills, trusts, power of attorney, beneficiary designations

### Banking & Financial Institutions
- **Commercial Banking**: Deposits, loans, mortgages; fractional reserve banking
- **Investment Banking**: Underwriting, M&A advisory, trading, research
- **Central Banks**: Monetary policy; lender of last resort; bank regulation
- **FinTech**: Digital payments, peer-to-peer lending, robo-advisors, blockchain, DeFi

## Key Theories

| Theory | Key Figure | Core Idea |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Modern Portfolio Theory | Markowitz | Diversification optimizes risk-return tradeoff |
| CAPM | Sharpe, Lintner | Expected return compensates for systematic risk |
| Efficient Market Hypothesis | Fama | Prices reflect all available information |
| Black-Scholes | Black, Scholes, Merton | Options pricing model |
| Modigliani-Miller | Modigliani, Miller | Capital structure irrelevance (with assumptions) |

- **John Bogle**: Index fund investing; Vanguard founder
