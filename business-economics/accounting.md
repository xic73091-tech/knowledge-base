# Accounting

## Overview

Accounting is the systematic process of recording, summarizing, analyzing, and reporting financial transactions. It provides the information foundation for business decision-making, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder communication. It is often called the "language of business."

## Core Concepts

### Financial Accounting
- **Accounting Equation**: Assets = Liabilities + Equity; double-entry bookkeeping
- **Financial Statements**:
  - **Balance Sheet**: Assets, liabilities, equity at a point in time
  - **Income Statement**: Revenue, expenses, net income over a period
  - **Cash Flow Statement**: Operating, investing, financing activities
  - **Statement of Changes in Equity**: Retained earnings, dividends, equity movements
- **Revenue Recognition**: When earned and realizable (ASC 606 / IFRS 15)
- **Accrual vs Cash Basis**: Revenue/expenses when incurred vs when cash changes hands
- **Depreciation & Amortization**: Allocating cost of tangible/intangible assets over useful life
- **GAAP & IFRS**: Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (US); International Financial Reporting Standards (global)

### Managerial/Cost Accounting
- **Cost Classification**: Fixed, variable, mixed; direct vs indirect; product vs period costs
- **Cost-Volume-Profit (CVP)**: Break-even analysis; contribution margin; operating leverage
- **Job Order vs Process Costing**: Custom vs mass production costing
- **Activity-Based Costing (ABC)**: Assigning overhead based on activities; cost drivers
- **Budgeting**: Operating budgets, capital budgets, cash budgets; variance analysis
- **Standard Costing**: Setting standards; analyzing variances (price, quantity, efficiency)

### Auditing
- **External Audit**: Independent examination of financial statements; opinion (unqualified, qualified, adverse, disclaimer)
- **Internal Audit**: Evaluating internal controls, risk management, governance
- **Audit Process**: Planning, risk assessment, substantive testing, reporting
- **Internal Controls**: COSO framework; segregation of duties; authorization; documentation
- **Fraud Detection**: Red flags; forensic accounting; Benford's law

### Taxation
- **Income Tax**: Individual and corporate; tax brackets; deductions; credits
- **Tax Planning**: Tax-efficient strategies; deferral; income splitting; tax-loss harvesting
- **Corporate Tax**: Effective vs statutory rates; tax shields; transfer pricing
- **Sales Tax & VAT**: Consumption taxes; collection and remittance
- **International Tax**: Double taxation treaties; BEPS (Base Erosion and Profit Shifting); tax havens
- **Tax Compliance**: Filing requirements; audits; penalties

### Accounting Information Systems
- **ERP Systems**: SAP, Oracle; integrated financial modules
- **General Ledger**: Chart of accounts; journal entries; trial balance
- **Accounts Receivable/Payable**: Customer invoicing; vendor payments; aging reports
- **Fixed Asset Management**: Asset register; depreciation schedules; disposal
- **Financial Reporting Software**: Automated reporting; consolidation; XBRL

### Advanced Topics
- **Consolidation**: Parent-subsidiary reporting; intercompany eliminations; minority interests
- **Leases**: Operating vs finance leases (ASC 842 / IFRS 16)
- **Financial Instruments**: Fair value; hedge accounting; derivatives
- **Impairment Testing**: Goodwill; long-lived assets; recoverable amount
- **Earnings Management**: Creative accounting; restatements; ethical considerations

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Double-Entry Bookkeeping | Every transaction affects at least two accounts |
| Matching Principle | Expenses matched to related revenues in same period |
| Conservatism | Anticipate losses, not gains; understatement over overstatement |
| Going Concern | Assume business will continue operating indefinitely |
| Materiality | Only information that affects decisions needs reporting |

## Important Figures

- **Luca Pacioli**: Father of accounting; described double-entry bookkeeping (1494)
- **William Paterson**: Early auditing standards
- **Henry Rand Hatfield**: Accounting theory pioneer
- **AICPA**: American Institute of CPAs; standard-setting
- **IASB**: International Accounting Standards Board; IFRS

## Applications

- **Public Accounting**: Audit, tax, advisory services (Big 4 firms)
- **Corporate Accounting**: Financial reporting, management accounting, treasury
- **Government Accounting**: Fund accounting, budgeting, accountability
- **Forensic Accounting**: Fraud investigation, litigation support, expert testimony
- **Tax Advisory**: Compliance, planning, international tax
- **Nonprofit Accounting**: Fund accounting, grant management, donor reporting
