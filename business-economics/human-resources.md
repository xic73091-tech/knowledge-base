# Human Resources Management

## Overview

Human resources (HR) management is the strategic approach to effectively managing people in organizations to achieve competitive advantage. It encompasses recruitment, development, compensation, retention, and compliance — the full employee lifecycle. Modern HR has evolved from administrative personnel management to a data-driven, strategic function central to organizational success.

## Core Concepts

### Talent Acquisition
- **Workforce Planning**: Forecasting staffing needs; gap analysis; succession planning
- **Job Analysis**: Defining roles, responsibilities, and competencies; job descriptions and specifications
- **Recruitment Channels**: Job boards, social media (LinkedIn), employee referrals, recruitment agencies, campus recruiting
- **Selection Methods**: Structured interviews, work samples, cognitive ability tests, personality assessments, assessment centers
- **Employer Branding**: Reputation as an employer; EVP (Employee Value Proposition); Glassdoor presence

### Compensation & Benefits
- **Base Pay**: Market-rate benchmarking; pay grades and bands; salary surveys
- **Variable Pay**: Bonuses, profit-sharing, stock options, commissions; pay-for-performance
- **Total Rewards**: Base + variable + benefits + non-monetary (flexibility, development, recognition)
- **Benefits**: Health insurance, retirement plans, paid leave, wellness programs
- **Pay Equity**: Gender pay gap, equal pay audits, transparent compensation practices

### Learning & Development
- **Onboarding**: Socialization, culture immersion, job-specific training; first 90 days
- **Training Needs Analysis**: Identifying skill gaps; competency frameworks
- **Delivery Methods**: Classroom, e-learning, on-the-job, coaching, mentoring, simulations
- **Leadership Development**: Succession pipelines; high-potential programs; 360-degree feedback
- **Career Development**: Career ladders and lattices; internal mobility; career pathing

### Performance Management
- **Goal Setting**: SMART goals; OKRs (Objectives and Key Results); alignment with organizational strategy
- **Performance Reviews**: Annual, quarterly, continuous feedback; calibration sessions
- **360-Degree Feedback**: Input from managers, peers, direct reports, self
- **Performance Improvement Plans**: Structured support for underperformers
- **Performance vs Development**: Separating evaluative and developmental conversations

### Employee Relations & Engagement
- **Employee Engagement**: Measuring satisfaction and commitment; pulse surveys; eNPS
- **Retention Strategies**: Career growth, compensation, culture, flexibility, recognition
- **Employee Voice**: Suggestion systems, town halls, skip-level meetings, unions
- **Conflict Resolution**: Mediation, grievance procedures, progressive discipline
- **Workplace Culture**: Values, norms, rituals; culture-fit vs culture-add hiring

### Employment Law & Compliance
- **Anti-Discrimination**: Title VII, ADA, ADEA; protected classes; disparate impact
- **Labor Law**: NLRA, collective bargaining, union organizing, unfair labor practices
- **Workplace Safety**: OSHA; hazard identification; workers' compensation
- **Leave & Accommodation**: FMLA, ADA reasonable accommodations, sick leave laws
- **Termination**: At-will employment, wrongful termination, severance, WARN Act

### HR Analytics & Technology
- **HRIS**: Human Resource Information Systems (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, BambooHR)
- **People Analytics**: Turnover prediction, engagement drivers, workforce planning models
- **Recruitment Technology**: Applicant tracking systems (ATS), AI screening, video interviews
- **Employee Experience Platforms**: Digital onboarding, recognition, communication tools
- **Metrics**: Time-to-hire, cost-per-hire, turnover rate, revenue per employee, engagement scores

### Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI)
- **Diversity**: Representation across dimensions (race, gender, age, disability, orientation, etc.)
- **Equity**: Fair processes and outcomes; removing systemic barriers
- **Inclusion**: Psychological safety; belonging; valuing diverse perspectives
- **Unconscious Bias**: Implicit associations affecting hiring, promotion, evaluation; training effectiveness debated
- **DEI Metrics**: Representation data, pay equity analyses, inclusion survey scores

### Strategic Workforce Planning
- **Talent Strategy**: Aligning workforce with business strategy; scenario planning
- **Succession Planning**: Identifying and developing internal successors for key roles
- **Workforce Analytics**: Predictive models for turnover, demand forecasting, skills gaps
- **Strategic Workforce Segmentation**: Critical roles, high potentials, bridge workers
- **Internal Talent Marketplaces**: AI-enabled platforms matching employees to projects
- **Mergers & Acquisitions**: Talent due diligence, cultural integration, retention

### Recruitment & Selection in Depth
- **Sourcing Strategies**: Boolean search, X-Ray search, talent communities, university relations
- **Structured Interviews**: Behaviorally anchored rating scales (BARS), STAR method, panel interviews
- **Assessment Methods**: Cognitive ability, personality (Big Five), emotional intelligence, work samples
- **Assessment Centers**: In-basket exercises, group discussions, role-plays, presentations
- **Background Checks**: Reference checks, criminal records, education verification, social media screening
- **Diversity Sourcing**: Targeted outreach, partnerships with diverse organizations
- **Candidate Experience**: Application process, communication, employer brand touchpoints

### Compensation & Benefits in Depth
- **Compensation Strategy**: Market positioning (lead, lag, match), pay philosophy, total compensation
- **Job Evaluation**: Point-factor, ranking, classification, market pricing
- **Salary Structures**: Pay grades, broadbanding, salary ranges; salary bands
- **Incentive Plans**: Short-term incentives (STI), long-term incentives (LTI), executive compensation
- **Sales Compensation**: Base, variable, accelerators, caps; SPIFs (special performance incentive funds)
- **Executive Compensation**: Base, bonus, equity (RSUs, options, PSUs), deferred compensation
- **Equity, Diversity & Pay**: Pay equity analyses, transparent pay practices, pay gap closure
- **Benefits Administration**: Health, retirement (401k, pension), paid leave, fringe benefits

### Learning & Development in Depth
- **Adult Learning Theory (Andragogy)**: Self-direction, experience, problem-centeredness
- **70-20-10 Model**: 70% on-the-job, 20% social, 10% formal learning
- **Instructional Design**: ADDIE (Analyze, Design, Develop, Implement, Evaluate), SAM, agile L&D
- **E-Learning Platforms**: LMS (Learning Management Systems), LXP (Learning Experience Platforms), MOOCs
- **Blended Learning**: Combining digital and in-person learning
- **Coaching & Mentoring**: Executive coaching, mentoring programs, peer coaching
- **Knowledge Management**: Communities of practice, knowledge bases, expertise location
- **Reskilling & Upskilling**: Skills-based learning, microlearning, credentialing
- **Leadership Development**: 360 feedback, executive education, action learning

### Performance Management in Depth
- **Continuous Performance Management**: Real-time feedback, check-ins, agile performance
- **Forced Ranking & Stack Ranking**: Top X%, middle, bottom (controversial practice)
- **9-Box Grid**: Performance vs potential for talent review
- **Goal Frameworks**: SMART, OKRs, BHAGs, MBO (Management by Objectives)
- **Calibration**: Cross-manager calibration to ensure consistency in ratings
- **Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)**: Documentation, support, timelines, outcomes
- **Managing Poor Performance**: Progressive discipline, documentation, offboarding
- **High-Performance Cultures**: Accountability, recognition, stretch assignments, learning

### Employee Experience & Engagement
- **Employee Experience (EX)**: Holistic experience from candidate to alumni
- **Employee Journey Mapping**: Touchpoints, moments that matter, pain points
- **Engagement Surveys**: Pulse surveys, annual surveys, eNPS, drivers analysis
- **Recognition Programs**: Peer-to-peer, manager-led, milestone celebrations
- **Total Worker Health**: Physical, mental, financial, social well-being
- **Flexible Work Arrangements**: Remote, hybrid, flexible hours, compressed workweeks
- **Sabbaticals & Leaves**: Sabbatical programs, parental leave, caregiver leave
- **Returnship Programs**: Re-entry programs for caregivers, retirees
- **Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)**: Affinity groups, community building, business impact

### Labor Relations & Employment Law
- **Unionization**: NLRB elections, collective bargaining, union avoidance
- **Collective Bargaining**: Negotiations, grievance procedures, contract administration
- **Labor-Management Cooperation**: Joint committees, partnership models
- **Employment Contracts**: Non-compete, non-solicitation, IP assignment, severance
- **Wrongful Termination**: At-will exceptions, whistleblower protections, discrimination
- **Workplace Investigations**: Harassment, discrimination, misconduct; investigator training
- **Workplace Safety**: OSHA, ergonomics, hazard communication, emergency preparedness
- **Global Mobility**: International assignments, immigration, tax, cultural training
- **Data Privacy in HR**: GDPR, CCPA, employee monitoring, biometric data

### HR Analytics & People Analytics
- **Workforce Metrics**: Turnover rate, time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, engagement scores
- **Predictive Analytics**: Flight risk, performance prediction, succession readiness
- **People Analytics Maturity**: Descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive
- **Talent Reviews**: 9-box, succession pools, retention risk analysis
- **HR Data Visualization**: Dashboards, storytelling, real-time analytics
- **Ethical People Analytics**: Privacy, bias, transparency, human oversight
- **Organizational Network Analysis (ONA)**: Communication patterns, informal influence

### HR Technology & Digital Transformation
- **Core HR Systems (HRIS)**: Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM Cloud, ADP
- **Talent Management Suites**: End-to-end talent lifecycle platforms
- **Recruitment Technology**: ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems), CRM, programmatic job advertising
- **Employee Experience Platforms**: Intranets, communication tools, recognition platforms
- **People Analytics Platforms**: Visier, OneModel, custom data warehouses
- **AI in HR**: Resume screening, chatbot assistants, predictive models, generative AI
- **HR Service Delivery**: Case management, knowledge bases, employee self-service

### Organizational Development & Change
- **Change Management Models**: Kotter's 8-step, ADKAR, McKinsey 7-S
- **Organization Design**: Structures, decision rights, span of control, layers
- **Culture Transformation**: Diagnosing culture, desired culture, change interventions
- **Team Effectiveness**: Team charters, team development (Tuckman), psychological safety
- **Leadership Development Pipelines**: From individual contributor to executive
- **M&A Integration**: Day 1 readiness, cultural integration, harmonization
- **Restructuring & Layoffs**: Layoff processes, WARN Act, outplacement, survivor support

### Future of Work
- **Remote Work**: Distributed teams, asynchronous work, remote management
- **Hybrid Work**: Office + remote balance, hot-desking, collaboration norms
- **Gig & Contingent Workforce**: Freelancers, contractors, platform workers, EOR (Employer of Record)
- **AI & Automation Impact**: Job displacement, augmentation, new roles
- **Skills-Based Organizations**: Moving from jobs to skills; dynamic talent allocation
- **Four-Day Work Week**: Pilot programs, productivity, well-being implications
- **Lifelong Learning**: Continuous reskilling, career agility, learning benefits
- **Mental Health & Well-Being**: EAPs, mental health days, manager training

### Globalization & Cross-Cultural HR
- **Global HR Strategy**: Localization vs standardization, talent mobility, global rewards
- **Cross-Cultural Competence**: Cultural intelligence (CQ), global leadership, expatriate training
- **International Compensation**: Hardship differentials, housing allowances, tax equalization
- **Global Mobility Policy**: Long-term assignments, short-term assignments, permanent transfers
- **Global Talent Management**: Identifying global leaders, developing global mindset
- **Employment Law in Different Jurisdictions**: Comparative labor law, works councils, employment contracts

### Sustainability, ESG & HR
- **Sustainability & HR**: Green HR, environmental training, sustainability metrics
- **ESG Reporting**: Workforce disclosures (diversity, pay gap, training hours)
- **Purpose-Driven Work**: Corporate purpose, social impact, employee engagement
- **Worker Well-Being as ESG**: Health, safety, well-being metrics
- **Supply Chain Labor Standards**: Vendor compliance, ethical sourcing, audits

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Human Capital Theory | Investment in employee skills and knowledge yields returns (Becker) |
| Herzberg's Two-Factor | Hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; motivators drive engagement |
| Psychological Contract | Implicit expectations between employer and employee; violation damages trust |
| AMO Framework | Performance = Ability × Motivation × Opportunity; all three needed |
| Expectancy Theory (Vroom) | Motivation = Expectancy × Instrumentality × Valence |
| Equity Theory (Adams) | Employees compare their input-outcome ratio with referents; perceived inequity drives tension |
| Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan) | Intrinsic motivation driven by autonomy, competence, relatedness |
| Job Demands-Resources Model | Burnout results from job demands exceeding job resources |
| High-Performance Work Systems | Bundles of HR practices that together create competitive advantage |
| Signaling Theory | Employers signal quality through HR practices; applicants signal through credentials |
