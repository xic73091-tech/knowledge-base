# Entrepreneurship

## Overview

Entrepreneurship is the process of designing, launching, and running a new business, often initially a small business, or the capacity and willingness to develop, organize, and manage a business venture along with any of its risks to make a profit. It is characterized by innovation, risk-taking, and the creation of new value.

## Core Concepts

### Ideation & Validation
- **Opportunity Recognition**: Identifying unmet needs, market gaps, emerging trends
- **Customer Discovery**: Problem interviews, solution interviews; understanding jobs-to-be-done
- **Lean Startup**: Build-Measure-Learn loop; MVP (Minimum Viable Product); pivot vs persevere
- **Design Thinking**: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test; human-centered design
- **Market Sizing**: TAM (Total Addressable Market), SAM, SOM; top-down and bottom-up estimation

### Business Models
- **Business Model Canvas**: 9 building blocks — value proposition, customer segments, channels, revenue streams, key resources, activities, partners, cost structure, customer relationships
- **Revenue Models**: Subscription, freemium, marketplace, advertising, licensing, SaaS, transactional
- **Platform Business Models**: Network effects; two-sided markets; chicken-and-egg problem
- **Social Enterprise**: Double/triple bottom line; impact measurement; B-Corp certification

### Funding & Finance
- **Bootstrapping**: Self-funding; revenue-based growth
- **Friends & Family**: Early-stage informal investment
- **Angel Investors**: High-net-worth individuals; seed stage; mentorship
- **Venture Capital**: Series A, B, C…; term sheets; valuation; dilution; board seats
- **Crowdfunding**: Reward-based (Kickstarter), equity-based, donation-based
- **Accelerators & Incubators**: Y Combinator, Techstars; mentorship, resources, demo days
- **Pitch Deck**: Problem, solution, market, traction, team, financials, ask
- **Term Sheets**: Pre-money valuation, liquidation preferences, anti-dilution, voting rights

### Building the Venture
- **Team Building**: Co-founder selection; complementary skills; equity splits; vesting
- **Product Development**: Agile development; user feedback; product-market fit
- **Go-to-Market Strategy**: Launch plan; customer acquisition; growth channels
- **Legal Formation**: Incorporation (C-Corp, LLC, S-Corp); IP protection; contracts
- **Culture & Values**: Defining culture early; hiring for culture fit; remote teams

### Growth & Scaling
- **Growth Metrics**: MRR/ARR, churn, CAC, LTV, burn rate, runway, growth rate
- **Growth Hacking**: Viral loops, referral programs, content marketing, SEO, partnerships
- **Scaling Challenges**: Hiring, process standardization, culture preservation, cash management
- **International Expansion**: Market entry strategies; localization; regulatory compliance
- **Pivoting**: Changing business model, market, or product based on learning

### Failure & Exit
- **Startup Failure**: Common causes — no market need, running out of cash, wrong team, competition
- **Failure Analysis**: Post-mortem; learning from failure; stigma reduction
- **Exit Strategies**: Acquisition, IPO, management buyout, liquidation
- **M&A Process**: Due diligence, valuation, integration planning

### Innovation & Intrapreneurship
- **Types of Innovation**: Incremental, disruptive (Christensen), radical, architectural
- **Disruptive Innovation**: Starts in low-end or new market; eventually displaces incumbents
- **Intrapreneurship**: Entrepreneurship within large organizations; innovation labs; skunkworks
- **Open Innovation**: External collaboration; crowdsourcing; partnerships

### Social Entrepreneurship
- **Social Ventures**: Addressing social/environmental problems through business
- **Impact Measurement**: Theory of change; SROI; impact metrics
- **Blended Finance**: Combining grants, debt, equity for social impact
- **Examples**: Grameen Bank, TOMS Shoes, Khan Academy, Teach for America

|--------|-----------|-----------|
