# Visual Arts

## Overview

Visual arts encompass the creation of works that are primarily visual in nature — including painting, sculpture, drawing, printmaking, photography, ceramics, and mixed media. They are concerned with aesthetic expression, conceptual communication, and the exploration of form, color, composition, and material.

## Core Concepts

### Drawing & Painting
- **Drawing Fundamentals**: Line, shape, form, value, texture, space; contour, gesture, cross-hatching
- **Perspective**: One-point, two-point, three-point; atmospheric perspective; foreshortening
- **Color Theory**: Color wheel; primary, secondary, tertiary colors; warm/cool; complementary, analogous, triadic harmonies
- **Color Properties**: Hue, value (lightness/darkness), saturation (intensity)
- **Painting Media**: Oil, acrylic, watercolor, gouache, tempera, fresco, encaustic
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, golden ratio, leading lines, focal point, balance (symmetrical/asymmetrical)
- **Techniques**: Glazing, impasto, scumbling, wet-on-wet, dry brush, alla prima

### Sculpture
- **Materials**: Stone, metal (bronze, steel), wood, clay, glass, resin, found objects
- **Techniques**: Carving (subtractive), modeling (additive), casting (lost wax, sand), welding, assemblage
- **Types**: Freestanding (in the round), relief (bas-relief, high-relief), kinetic, light-based
- **Installation**: Site-specific; immersive environments; mixed media
- **Contemporary Approaches**: Conceptual sculpture; social practice; ephemeral works

### Photography
- **Technical Foundations**: Exposure (aperture, shutter speed, ISO); focal length; depth of field
- **Camera Types**: DSLR, mirrorless, medium format, large format, smartphone
- **Genres**: Portrait, landscape, street, documentary, fashion, product, fine art, astrophotography
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, symmetry, negative space
- **Post-Processing**: RAW development; Adobe Lightroom, Photoshop; color grading; retouching
- **History**: Daguerreotype → film → digital; key figures (Daguerre, Adams, Cartier-Bresson, Arbus)

### Printmaking
- **Relief Printing**: Woodcut, linocut; ink applied to raised surface
- **Intaglio**: Engraving, etching, aquatint, mezzotint; ink in incised lines
- **Lithography**: Drawing on stone or plate; chemical process
- **Screen Printing (Serigraphy)**: Stencil-based; Andy Warhol; commercial and fine art
- **Digital Printing**: Giclée; large format; archival quality

### Ceramics
- **Hand-Building**: Pinch, coil, slab techniques
- **Wheel Throwing**: Centering, opening, pulling, trimming
- **Glazing & Firing**: Glaze chemistry; bisque firing, glaze firing; kiln types (electric, gas, wood, raku)
- **Functional vs Sculptural**: Pottery (tableware, vessels) vs ceramic sculpture

### Contemporary & New Media
- **Conceptual Art**: Idea over object; instruction-based; text-based
- **Performance Art**: Body as medium; duration; audience interaction; Abramović, Acconci
- **Video Art**: Time-based; single channel, multi-channel, installation
- **Digital Art**: Generative art; AI art; NFTs; net art; virtual reality
- **Street Art & Public Art**: Murals, graffiti, interventions; Banksy, JR, KAWS
- **Socially Engaged Art**: Community collaboration; participatory practice; social impact

### Art Practice & Career
- **Studio Practice**: Developing a body of work; artist statement; studio habits
- **Portfolio**: Curating work; digital and physical portfolios; artist websites
- **Exhibiting**: Galleries, museums, art fairs, biennials, open studios
- **Art Market**: Primary market (galleries), secondary market (auctions); pricing; representation
- **Residencies & Grants**: Artist-in-residence programs; funding opportunities
- **Critique & Feedback**: Constructive criticism; peer review; MFA critiques

## Key Theories

| Theory | Key Figure | Core Idea |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Formalism | Greenberg | Art valued for formal qualities (color, line, form) |
| Institutional Theory | Dickie, Danto | Art defined by art world context |
| Relational Aesthetics | Bourriaud | Art as social interaction |
| Aura of Artwork | Benjamin | Original artwork has unique presence; reproduction diminishes it |

## Important Figures

- **Leonardo da Vinci**: Universal genius; painting, sculpture, anatomy, engineering
- **Michelangelo**: Sistine Chapel; David; architecture
- **Rembrandt**: Master of light and shadow; self-portraits
- **Pablo Picasso**: Cubism; prolific across media
- **Frida Kahlo**: Surrealist self-portraits; Mexican identity
- **Andy Warhol**: Pop Art; mass culture; The Factory
- **Marina Abramović**: Performance art pioneer
- **Ai Weiwei**: Contemporary Chinese art; political activism

## Applications

- **Fine Art**: Gallery representation, museum exhibitions, art market
- **Illustration**: Book, editorial, medical, scientific illustration
- **Art Therapy**: Therapeutic use of art-making; mental health treatment
- **Public Art**: Murals, sculptures, installations in public spaces
- **Art Education**: Teaching, workshops, community programs
- **Conservation**: Preserving and restoring artworks
