# Graphic Design

## Overview

Graphic design is the art and practice of planning and projecting ideas and experiences with visual and textual content. It communicates messages through typography, photography, iconography, and illustration, applied across print, digital, environmental, and interactive media.

## Core Concepts

### Typography
- **Type Anatomy**: Baseline, x-height, cap height, ascender, descender, serif, counter
- **Typeface Classification**: Serif (old style, transitional, modern), sans-serif, slab serif, script, display, monospace
- **Hierarchy**: Size, weight, color, spacing to guide the eye; headings, subheadings, body text
- **Kerning, Tracking, Leading**: Space between pairs, overall letter spacing, line spacing
- **Readability & Legibility**: Font size, contrast, line length (45-75 characters), line spacing
- **Variable Fonts**: Responsive typography; weight, width, slant axes
- **Type Selection**: Matching type to tone; pairing typefaces; system fonts vs custom

### Layout & Composition
- **Grid Systems**: Column grids, modular grids, hierarchical grids; baseline grid
- **White Space (Negative Space)**: Breathing room; visual hierarchy; elegance
- **Alignment**: Left, center, right, justified; edge alignment
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Size, color, contrast, position, spacing to prioritize information
- **Rule of Thirds / Golden Ratio**: Compositional guides for balance
- **Gestalt Principles**: Proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, figure-ground

### Color Theory (Applied)
- **Color Models**: RGB (screen), CMYK (print), HSB/HSL, Pantone (spot colors)
- **Color Psychology**: Cultural associations; warm/cool; emotional responses
- **Color Accessibility**: Contrast ratios (WCAG); color blindness considerations
- **Color Palettes**: Monochromatic, analogous, complementary, triadic, split-complementary
- **Brand Colors**: Primary, secondary, accent; color systems; style guides

### Visual Identity & Branding
- **Logo Design**: Wordmarks, lettermarks, pictorial marks, abstract marks, mascots, emblems
- **Brand Identity System**: Logo, colors, typography, imagery, iconography, voice
- **Brand Guidelines**: Usage rules; minimum sizes; clear space; do's and don'ts
- **Stationery & Collateral**: Business cards, letterheads, envelopes, presentations
- **Rebranding**: Refresh vs complete overhaul; rationale; stakeholder management

### Print Design
- **Editorial Design**: Magazines, books, newspapers; layout spreads; folios
- **Poster Design**: Hierarchy; impact; limited space; event posters, advertising
- **Packaging Design**: Structure (boxes, bottles, bags); labeling; shelf appeal; dielines
- **Brochure & Flyer**: Bi-fold, tri-fold, Z-fold; information architecture
- **Print Production**: Bleed, trim, safe area; spot colors; paper stocks; binding; finishing

### Digital Design
- **Web Design**: Responsive design; layouts; wireframes; mockups; design systems
- **UI Design**: Interface elements; buttons, forms, navigation, cards; consistency
- **UX Design**: User research; personas; user flows; information architecture; usability testing
- **Design Systems**: Component libraries; style tokens; Figma/Sketch libraries; atomic design
- **Social Media Graphics**: Post templates, stories, banners, ads; platform-specific sizes
- **Email Design**: Layout; responsiveness; CTA design; dark mode compatibility

### Illustration & Imagery
- **Illustration Styles**: Flat, isometric, 3D, hand-drawn, watercolor, digital painting
- **Icon Design**: Pixel-perfect; consistent style; pictograms; icon systems
- **Photography in Design**: Art direction; stock vs custom; photo editing for design
- **Data Visualization**: Charts, graphs, infographics; information design; clarity and accuracy
- **Motion Graphics**: Animated logos, UI animations, explainer videos, social media animations

### Tools & Software
- **Adobe Creative Suite**: Photoshop (raster), Illustrator (vector), InDesign (layout), After Effects (motion)
- **Figma**: Collaborative UI/UX design; prototyping; design systems
- **Sketch**: UI design (macOS); plugins; symbols
- **Canva**: Accessible design; templates; non-designers
- **Procreate**: Digital illustration (iPad)
- **Blender/Cinema 4D**: 3D design; product visualization; motion

### Design Process
- **Brief & Research**: Understanding the problem; audience analysis; competitive research
- **Ideation**: Moodboards, sketches, thumbnails; exploring directions
- **Concept Development**: Refining chosen direction; design rationale
- **Execution**: Pixel-perfect production; file preparation; specs
- **Presentation**: Design rationale; stakeholder feedback; iteration
- **Handoff**: Developer handoff; production files; style guides

## Key Theories

| Theory | Key Figure | Core Idea |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Less is More | Mies van der Rohe | Simplicity and restraint in design |
| Swiss/International Style | Müller-Brockmann | Grid systems; clean typography; objectivity |
| Gestalt Principles | Wertheimer, Koffka | Visual perception of wholes vs parts |
| Wabi-Sabi | Japanese aesthetics | Beauty in imperfection and transience |

## Important Figures

- **Jan Tschichold**: New Typography; Penguin Books design
- **Paul Rand**: IBM, UPS, ABC logos; graphic design pioneer
- **Massimo Vignelli**: NYC subway map; Helvetica; modernist design
- **Sheila Levrant de Bretteville**: Feminist design; Yale professor
- **David Carson**: Experimental typography; Ray Gun magazine
- **Stefan Sagmeister**: Conceptual design; Sagmeister & Walsh
- **Jessica Walsh**: Contemporary design; social media presence
