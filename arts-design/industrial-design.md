# Industrial Design

## Overview

Industrial design is the professional practice of designing products, devices, and objects that are manufactured through mass production. It combines art, business, and engineering to create products that are functional, aesthetically appealing, ergonomic, and commercially viable. Industrial designers consider user needs, manufacturing processes, materials, sustainability, and market trends to develop products that enhance people's lives while being feasible to produce at scale. The field spans consumer electronics, furniture, automobiles, medical devices, and virtually every manufactured product.
