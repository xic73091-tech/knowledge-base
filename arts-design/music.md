# Music

## Overview

Music is the art of organizing sound in time through elements such as melody, harmony, rhythm, timbre, and dynamics. It is a universal human cultural activity that exists in every known society. Music encompasses composition, performance, theory, history, technology, and ethnomusicology.

## Core Concepts

### Music Theory
- **Pitch & Scales**: Major, minor (natural, harmonic, melodic), pentatonic, chromatic, whole-tone, modes
- **Intervals**: Unison, second, third… octave; perfect, major, minor, augmented, diminished
- **Chords**: Triads (major, minor, diminished, augmented), seventh chords, extended chords (9th, 11th, 13th)
- **Harmony**: Chord progressions; cadences (authentic, plagal, half, deceptive); functional harmony (tonic, subdominant, dominant)
- **Rhythm & Meter**: Time signatures (4/4, 3/4, 6/8); syncopation; polyrhythm; metric modulation
- **Notation**: Staff, clef, key signature, time signature, dynamics, articulation, tempo markings
- **Counterpoint**: Voice leading; species counterpoint; fugue (Bach)
- **Form**: Binary (AB), ternary (ABA), rondo (ABACA), sonata-allegro, theme and variations, through-composed

### Composition & Songwriting
- **Melody Writing**: Contour, motifs, sequences, development, phrasing
- **Harmonic Language**: Chord voicings, substitutions, modulation, modal interchange
- **Lyric Writing**: Rhyme schemes, meter, prosody, storytelling, imagery
- **Arranging**: Orchestration, instrumentation, scoring for ensembles
- **Song Structure**: Verse-chorus, AABA, verse-chorus-bridge, intro/outro

### Performance
- **Vocal**: Technique, breath control, vibrato, belting, falsetto, head voice; opera, choral, pop
- **Piano/Keyboard**: Technique, repertoire, accompaniment, sight-reading
- **Guitar**: Acoustic, electric; fingerpicking, strumming, lead, rhythm; genres
- **Orchestral Instruments**: Strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion; orchestral seating
- **Conducting**: Baton technique; score study; rehearsal techniques; interpretation
- **Ensemble Types**: Orchestra, chamber music, jazz combo, rock band, choir, marching band

### Music History & Genres
- **Classical**: Medieval, Renaissance, Baroque (Bach, Handel), Classical (Mozart, Haydn), Romantic (Beethoven, Chopin, Brahms), 20th Century (Stravinsky, Schoenberg, Cage)
- **Jazz**: New Orleans, swing, bebop (Parker, Coltrane), cool jazz, free jazz, fusion
- **Rock & Pop**: Rock and roll, British Invasion, psychedelic, punk, new wave, alternative, indie, pop
- **Blues & R&B**: Delta blues, Chicago blues, soul, funk, Motown, contemporary R&B
- **Hip-Hop & Rap**: Origins (DJ Kool Herc, Grandmaster Flash), golden age, trap, conscious hip-hop
- **Electronic**: House, techno, trance, dubstep, ambient, IDM, lo-fi
- **World Music**: African drumming, Indian classical (raga, tala), Latin (salsa, bossa nova, reggaeton), Middle Eastern (maqam), Celtic, Chinese traditional
- **Country & Folk**: Appalachian, bluegrass, honky-tonk, Americana, singer-songwriter

### Music Technology
- **Recording**: Microphone types (dynamic, condenser, ribbon); recording techniques; DAWs (Logic, Ableton, Pro Tools)
- **Mixing**: Levels, panning, EQ, compression, reverb, delay; mixing console; automation
- **Mastering**: Final processing; loudness; streaming standards; LUFS
- **Synthesis**: Subtractive, additive, FM, wavetable, granular, physical modeling
- **Sampling**: Sample libraries; sampling ethics; remixing
- **Music Production**: Beat-making; arrangement; sound design; workflow
- **Live Sound**: PA systems; monitor mixing; stage setup; sound reinforcement

### Music Business
- **Copyright & Publishing**: Mechanical, performance, sync rights; publishing deals
- **Record Labels**: Major (Universal, Sony, Warner) and independent; distribution
- **Streaming**: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube; royalty structures; playlist placement
- **Live Performance**: Booking; touring; venue types; festival circuit
- **Music Marketing**: Social media, branding, fan engagement, press, playlists

### Ethnomusicology
- **Fieldwork**: Recording, documentation, participant observation
- **Organology**: Classification of instruments (Hornbostel-Sachs)
- **Music & Identity**: Ethnic, national, gender, class identities through music
- **Music & Ritual**: Religious ceremonies, healing, rites of passage
- **Globalization**: World music; fusion; appropriation vs appreciation

## Key Theories

| Theory | Key Figure | Core Idea |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Functional Harmony | Rameau | Chords have functional roles (tonic, dominant, subdominant) |
| Twelve-Tone Technique | Schoenberg | Serialism; all 12 chromatic pitches equal |
| Aleatoric Music | Cage | Chance operations in composition |
| Minimalism | Reich, Glass, Riley | Repetition, gradual processes, pulse |

- **Ludwig van Beethoven**: Bridge Classical-Romantic; symphonies, sonatas
- **Louis Armstrong**: Jazz trumpet; vocal improvisation
