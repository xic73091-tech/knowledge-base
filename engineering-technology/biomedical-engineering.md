# Biomedical Engineering

## Overview

Biomedical engineering applies engineering principles and design concepts to medicine and biology for healthcare purposes. It bridges engineering and medical sciences, developing devices, systems, and procedures that solve medical problems and improve patient care.

## Core Concepts

### Biomechanics
- **Statics & Dynamics of Body**: Force analysis of joints, muscles, bones
- **Gait Analysis**: Walking mechanics; kinematics and kinetics of movement
- **Orthopedic Biomechanics**: Bone stress-strain behavior; fracture mechanics; implant design
- **Cardiovascular Biomechanics**: Blood flow dynamics; vessel wall mechanics; heart valve design
- **Injury Biomechanics**: Crash injury mechanisms; head trauma; protective equipment design

### Biomaterials
- **Material Classes**: Metals (titanium, cobalt-chrome), polymers (PEEK, silicone), ceramics (hydroxyapatite), composites
- **Biocompatibility**: Foreign body response; cytotoxicity; ISO 10993 testing
- **Implant Materials**: Hip/knee replacements, dental implants, stents, pacemaker leads
- **Surface Modification**: Coatings, plasma treatment, drug-eluting surfaces
- **Degradable Materials**: Absorbable sutures, drug delivery scaffolds

### Medical Imaging
- **X-Ray**: Projection radiography; CT (computed tomography) — 3D cross-sectional imaging
- **MRI**: Magnetic resonance; soft tissue contrast; functional MRI (fMRI) for brain activity
- **Ultrasound**: Doppler for blood flow; echocardiography; point-of-care ultrasound
- **Nuclear Medicine**: PET (positron emission tomography); SPECT; radioisotope tracers
- **Optical Imaging**: Endoscopy, OCT (optical coherence tomography), fluorescence imaging
- **Image Processing**: Segmentation, registration, reconstruction; AI-assisted diagnosis

### Biomedical Devices
- **Diagnostic Devices**: Blood glucose monitors, pulse oximeters, ECG, EEG
- **Therapeutic Devices**: Pacemakers, defibrillators, insulin pumps, ventilators
- **Surgical Tools**: Laparoscopic instruments, robotic surgery (da Vinci), laser surgery
- **Prosthetics & Orthotics**: Artificial limbs, exoskeletons, spinal braces
- **Wearable Health Tech**: Fitness trackers, continuous glucose monitors, smartwatches

### Tissue Engineering & Regenerative Medicine
- **Scaffolds**: Biodegradable matrices for cell growth; 3D bioprinting
- **Stem Cells**: Embryonic, induced pluripotent (iPSC), adult stem cells
- **Growth Factors**: Signaling molecules that promote tissue regeneration
- **Organ-on-a-Chip**: Microfluidic devices mimicking organ function
- **Gene Therapy**: CRISPR-based approaches; viral vectors; CAR-T cell therapy

### Bioinformatics & Computational Biology
- **Genomics**: DNA sequencing; genome assembly; variant calling
- **Protein Structure**: AlphaFold; molecular dynamics; protein folding
- **Systems Biology**: Network modeling; pathway analysis
- **Clinical Informatics**: Electronic health records; clinical decision support
- **Machine Learning in Medicine**: Diagnostic imaging AI, drug discovery, genomics

### Rehabilitation Engineering
- **Assistive Technology**: Wheelchairs, communication devices, environmental control
- **Neural Interfaces**: Brain-computer interfaces; cochlear implants; retinal prostheses
- **Physical Therapy Devices**: Robotic rehabilitation, electrical stimulation, balance training
- **Accessibility Design**: Universal design principles; ADA compliance

### Clinical Engineering
- **Medical Device Regulation**: FDA (US), CE (EU), NMPA (China); 510(k), PMA pathways
- **Hospital Equipment Management**: Maintenance, calibration, safety testing
- **Risk Management**: ISO 14971; hazard analysis; failure mode effects analysis
- **Sterilization**: Autoclave, ethylene oxide, gamma radiation, E-beam

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Hill's Muscle Model | Force-velocity relationship in muscle |
| Poiseuille's Law | Blood flow proportional to radius⁴ |
| Wolff's Law | Bone remodels in response to mechanical loading |
| Hodgkin-Huxley Model | Electrical behavior of neurons |

## Applications

- **Medical Devices**: Diagnostic imaging, surgical tools, implantable devices
- **Prosthetics & Implants**: Joint replacements, cochlear implants, cardiac devices
- **Drug Delivery**: Controlled release, nanoparticles, targeted therapy
- **Diagnostics**: Lab-on-a-chip, biosensors, AI-powered diagnosis
- **Rehabilitation**: Assistive devices, robotic therapy, neural interfaces
- **Pharmaceutical Industry**: Bioprocess engineering, quality control
