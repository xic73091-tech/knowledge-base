# Aerospace Engineering

## Overview

Aerospace engineering is the primary field of engineering concerned with the development of aircraft and spacecraft. It encompasses two major overlapping branches: aeronautical engineering (atmospheric flight) and astronautical engineering (space flight).

## Core Concepts

### Aerodynamics
- **Fluid Properties**: Density, viscosity, compressibility; Reynolds number
- **Airfoil Theory**: Lift generation; pressure distribution; camber; angle of attack
- **Boundary Layer**: Laminar vs turbulent; separation; drag
- **Drag Types**: Parasitic (form, friction, interference) and induced (lift-dependent)
- **Compressible Flow**: Mach number; shock waves; supersonic/hypersonic aerodynamics
- **Wind Tunnel Testing**: Subsonic, transonic, supersonic, hypersonic facilities
- **CFD**: Computational fluid dynamics; turbulence modeling (RANS, LES, DNS)

### Flight Mechanics
- **Four Forces**: Lift, weight, thrust, drag; equilibrium conditions
- **Stability & Control**: Static and dynamic stability; control surfaces (ailerons, elevators, rudder)
- **Performance**: Range, endurance, rate of climb, ceiling, takeoff/landing distance
- **Flight Envelope**: V-n diagram; maneuvering; load factor
- **Navigation**: Inertial navigation; GPS; VOR/ILS

### Propulsion
- **Jet Engines**: Turbojet, turbofan, turboprop, turboshaft; Brayton cycle
- **Rocket Propulsion**: Liquid, solid, hybrid rockets; specific impulse; Tsiolkovsky equation
- **Ramjet/Scramjet**: Supersonic combustion; no moving parts
- **Electric Propulsion**: Ion thrusters, Hall effect thrusters; high Isp, low thrust
- **Turbomachinery**: Compressor and turbine stages; blade design

### Structures & Materials
- **Aerospace Materials**: Aluminum alloys, titanium, carbon fiber composites, ceramic matrix composites
- **Stiffened Panels**: Skin-stringer construction; sandwich structures
- **Fatigue & Damage Tolerance**: Crack growth; inspection intervals; safe-life vs damage-tolerant design
- **Finite Element Analysis**: Stress analysis; modal analysis; aeroelasticity
- **Thermal Protection**: Heat shields; ablative materials; thermal management

### Orbital Mechanics
- **Kepler's Laws**: Elliptical orbits; equal areas in equal times; period-radius relationship
- **Orbital Elements**: Semi-major axis, eccentricity, inclination, RAAN, argument of perigee
- **Hohmann Transfer**: Minimum-energy orbit transfer
- **Orbital Maneuvers**: Plane changes; rendezvous; docking
- **Lagrange Points**: Five equilibrium points in three-body problem
- **Reentry**: Ballistic, skip, lifting reentry; heating; deceleration

### Spacecraft Systems
- **Attitude Control**: Reaction wheels, control moment gyroscopes, thrusters, magnetorquers
- **Power Systems**: Solar panels, batteries, radioisotope thermoelectric generators (RTGs)
- **Thermal Control**: Passive (insulation, coatings) and active (heat pipes, heaters)
- **Communications**: Deep space network; antenna design; link budget
- **Life Support**: ECLSS (Environmental Control and Life Support System); oxygen, water recycling

### Avionics & Systems
- **Flight Control Systems**: Fly-by-wire; redundant systems; autopilot
- **Radar & Sensors**: Weather radar, terrain-following radar, synthetic aperture radar
- **Navigation Systems**: INS, GPS, star trackers
- **Mission Planning**: Trajectory optimization; payload integration

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Bernoulli's Principle | Faster flow → lower pressure; explains lift |
| Navier-Stokes | Fundamental equations of fluid dynamics |
| Thin Airfoil Theory | Theoretical lift prediction for airfoils |
| Keplerian Orbital Mechanics | Two-body orbital motion |
| Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation | Δv = Isp × g₀ × ln(m₀/mf) |

## Important Figures

- **Wright Brothers**: First powered, sustained flight (1903)
- **Theodore von Kármán**: Aerodynamics; supersonic flight
- **Robert Goddard**: Liquid-fueled rocket pioneer
- **Wernher von Braun**: Saturn V; V-2 rocket
- **Yuri Gagarin**: First human in space (1961)
- **Neil Armstrong**: First human on Moon (1969)
- **Elon Musk**: SpaceX; reusable rockets

## Frontiers

- **Sustainable Aviation Fuels and Hydrogen Flight**: Decarbonizing air travel through SAF drop-in fuels, liquid hydrogen combustion, and hydrogen fuel cell propulsion
- **Hypersonic Vehicle Design**: Solving thermal protection, scramjet propulsion, and materials challenges for sustained Mach 5+ flight
- **Autonomous Urban Air Mobility**: Certifying eVTOL aircraft and air taxi operations in dense urban environments with detect-and-avoid systems
- **Space Debris Mitigation**: Active debris removal, on-orbit servicing, and designing spacecraft for end-of-life deorbiting
- **In-Space Manufacturing and Assembly**: Building large structures (antennas, habitats) in orbit rather than launching them fully assembled

## Applications

- **Commercial Aviation**: Airliner design; air traffic management
- **Military**: Fighter jets, bombers, UAVs, missiles
- **Space Exploration**: Robotic probes, space stations, Mars missions
- **Satellite Systems**: Communication, GPS, Earth observation, weather
- **Urban Air Mobility**: eVTOL, air taxis, drone delivery
- **Defense**: Missile defense, surveillance, reconnaissance

## Classic Works

- **"Anderson's Fundamentals of Aerodynamics"** by John D. Anderson Jr. — The standard aerodynamics textbook; covers incompressible to hypersonic flow with physical intuition
- **"Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students"** by Curtis — Clear introduction to Keplerian orbits, orbital maneuvers, and mission design
- **"Rocket Propulsion Elements"** by Sutton & Biblarz — Definitive reference on chemical rocket design, nozzles, and performance analysis
- **"Aircraft Structures for Engineering Students"** by Megson — Covers stress analysis, fatigue, and aeroelasticity specific to airframes
- **"Mechanics of Flight"** by Barnard & Philpott — Accessible treatment of flight performance, stability, and control for both aircraft and spacecraft
