# Chemical Engineering

## Overview

Chemical engineering applies principles of chemistry, physics, mathematics, and biology to the design, optimization, and operation of processes that convert raw materials into useful products. It bridges laboratory chemistry and industrial-scale production.

## Core Concepts

### Transport Phenomena
- **Fluid Mechanics**: Pipe flow, pumps, mixing; non-Newtonian fluids; multiphase flow
- **Heat Transfer**: Conduction, convection, radiation; heat exchangers; heat exchanger networks
- **Mass Transfer**: Diffusion (Fick's law); convective mass transfer; mass transfer coefficients
- **Analogies**: Momentum, heat, and mass transfer share similar mathematical frameworks

### Thermodynamics
- **Phase Equilibria**: Vapor-liquid equilibrium (VLE); Raoult's law; activity coefficients
- **Equations of State**: Ideal gas, van der Waals, Peng-Robinson
- **Chemical Equilibrium**: Gibbs free energy; equilibrium constants; Le Chatelier's principle
- **Properties Estimation**: Critical properties; corresponding states; group contribution methods

### Reaction Engineering
- **Kinetics**: Rate laws; Arrhenius equation; activation energy; reaction orders
- **Reactor Design**: CSTR (continuous stirred-tank), PFR (plug flow), batch reactors
- **Catalysis**: Homogeneous, heterogeneous; enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten)
- **Residence Time Distribution**: Mixing patterns; reactor models
- **Multiple Reactions**: Selectivity, yield, conversion optimization

### Separation Processes
- **Distillation**: McCabe-Thiele method; flash, batch, continuous; azeotropes
- **Absorption & Stripping**: Gas-liquid mass transfer; tower design
- **Extraction**: Liquid-liquid; solid-liquid (leaching)
- **Membrane Separation**: Reverse osmosis, ultrafiltration, gas permeation
- **Crystallization**: Nucleation, crystal growth; purity control
- **Adsorption**: Activated carbon, zeolites; breakthrough curves
- **Chromatography**: Preparative and industrial scale

### Process Design & Control
- **Process Flow Diagrams (PFD)**: Stream tables; equipment specifications
- **Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&ID)**: Valves, instruments, control loops
- **Process Simulation**: Aspen, HYSYS; steady-state and dynamic modeling
- **Process Control**: Feedback, feedforward, cascade control; PID controllers
- **Process Safety**: HAZOP; fault tree analysis; relief systems; inherently safer design
- **Optimization**: Linear/nonlinear programming; pinch analysis; energy integration

### Materials & Polymers
- **Polymerization**: Addition, condensation; molecular weight distribution
- **Polymer Properties**: Glass transition, crystallinity, viscoelasticity
- **Rheology**: Flow behavior of complex fluids
- **Corrosion**: Electrochemical mechanisms; prevention methods

### Biochemical Engineering
- **Fermentation**: Microbial growth kinetics; bioreactor design
- **Downstream Processing**: Cell harvesting, product purification
- **Enzyme Engineering**: Immobilization; enzyme reactors
- **Biopharmaceuticals**: Protein production; cell culture; monoclonal antibodies

## Key Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Conservation Laws | Mass, energy, momentum balances on any system |
| Fick's Law | Mass transfer driven by concentration gradient |
| Arrhenius Equation | Reaction rate exponential in temperature |

## Frontiers

- **Electrochemical Process Intensification**: Replacing thermal separation with membrane electrolysis and electrochemical reactors to cut industrial energy use
- **Continuous Pharmaceutical Manufacturing**: Shifting drug production from batch to continuous flow for quality, speed, and cost advantages
- **Plastic Upcycling**: Chemically converting waste plastics into monomers, fuels, or high-value chemicals at industrial scale
- **AI-Guided Process Optimization**: Using reinforcement learning and digital twins to autonomously tune plant operations in real time
- **Green Hydrogen at Scale**: Developing cost-effective electrolysis and storage systems to decarbonize steel, ammonia, and refining

## Applications

- **Petrochemicals**: Refining, cracking, reforming; fuels, lubricants
- **Pharmaceuticals**: Drug synthesis, purification, formulation
- **Food Processing**: Pasteurization, drying, fermentation, preservation
- **Materials**: Polymers, ceramics, semiconductors, nanomaterials
- **Energy**: Biofuels, hydrogen production, battery manufacturing
- **Environmental**: Pollution control, waste treatment, carbon capture

## Classic Works

- **"Transport Phenomena"** by Bird, Stewart & Lightfoot — The foundational text linking momentum, heat, and mass transfer through rigorous mathematical treatment
- **"Chemical Reaction Engineering"** by Levenspiel — Classic treatment of reactor design, kinetics, and residence time distributions
- **"Unit Operations of Chemical Engineering"** by McCabe, Smith & Harriott — Comprehensive reference for distillation, absorption, extraction, and other separation processes
- **"Elementary Principles of Chemical Processes"** by Felder & Rousseau — Excellent introductory text for material and energy balances
- **"Separation Process Principles"** by Seader, Henley & Roper — Detailed coverage of modern separation techniques including membranes and chromatography
