# Urban Studies

## Overview

Urban studies is an interdisciplinary field that examines cities, urban life, and the process of urbanization. It draws on sociology, geography, economics, political science, anthropology, and planning to understand how cities function, how urban spaces are produced and experienced, and how urbanization shapes social, economic, political, and environmental dynamics. With more than half the world's population now living in urban areas, urban studies addresses critical challenges of inequality, sustainability, governance, and quality of life in an increasingly urban world.
