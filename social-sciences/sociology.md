# Sociology

## Overview

Sociology is the scientific study of society, patterns of social relationships, social interaction, and aspects of culture associated with everyday life. It ranges from micro-level individual interactions to macro-level systems and social structures.

## Core Concepts

### Social Structure & Institutions
- **Social Institutions**: Family, education, religion, economy, government, healthcare
- **Social Roles**: Expected behavior patterns attached to social positions
- **Status**: Ascribed (born into) vs achieved (earned); status sets
- **Social Networks**: Ties, weak ties (Granovetter), structural holes, social capital
- **Organizations**: Bureaucracy (Weber), organizational culture, institutional isomorphism

### Social Stratification
- **Class Systems**: Upper, middle, working, underclass; socioeconomic status (SES)
- **Social Mobility**: Intergenerational, intragenerational; barriers to mobility
- **Inequality**: Income, wealth, health, educational disparities
- **Caste & Estate Systems**: Historical stratification systems
- **Intersectionality**: Race, class, gender, sexuality interact to shape experience (Crenshaw)

### Culture
- **Material vs Non-Material Culture**: Objects vs beliefs, values, norms
- **Cultural Capital**: Knowledge, skills, education as social resource (Bourdieu)
- **Hegemony**: Dominant group's worldview as "natural" (Gramsci)
- **Subcultures & Countercultures**: Groups with distinct cultural patterns
- **Cultural Diffusion**: Spread of cultural elements between societies

### Socialization & Identity
- **Primary Socialization**: Family; early childhood learning
- **Secondary Socialization**: School, peers, media, workplace
- **Self & Identity**: Looking-glass self (Cooley); dramaturgy (Goffman); identity theory
- **Agents of Socialization**: Family, school, peer groups, media, religion

### Deviance & Social Control
- **Deviance**: Behavior violating social norms; not necessarily criminal
- **Theories**: Strain theory (Merton), labeling theory, differential association, social bond theory
- **Social Control**: Formal (laws, police) and informal (norms, shame, gossip)
- **Criminal Justice**: Policing, courts, punishment, rehabilitation

### Demography & Population
- **Population Growth**: Malthusian theory; demographic transition model
- **Migration**: Push-pull factors; urbanization; diaspora
- **Fertility & Mortality**: Birth rates, death rates, life expectancy
- **Urbanization**: Rural-to-urban migration; megacities; suburbanization

### Social Change
- **Modernization**: Industrialization, rationalization, secularization
- **Globalization**: Economic, cultural, political interconnectedness
- **Social Movements**: Collective action, resource mobilization, framing
- **Revolution**: Conditions, processes, outcomes

## Key Theories

| Theory | Key Figure | Core Idea |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Functionalism | Durkheim | Society as interconnected system; each part serves a function |
| Conflict Theory | Marx | Society driven by inequality and power struggles |
| Symbolic Interactionism | Mead, Blumer | Meaning created through social interaction |
| Structural Functionalism | Parsons, Merton | Social structures maintain stability and order |
| Postmodernism | Baudrillard, Lyotard | Skepticism of grand narratives; fragmentation |
| Rational Choice | Coleman | Individuals make calculated decisions to maximize benefit |

## Important Figures

- **Émile Durkheim**: Social facts; solidarity; suicide study
- **Karl Marx**: Class conflict; historical materialism
- **Max Weber**: Verstehen; bureaucracy; Protestant ethic
- **Georg Simmel**: Social forms; the stranger; urban sociology
- **W.E.B. Du Bois**: Race sociology; double consciousness
- **Erving Goffman**: Dramaturgy; stigma; frame analysis
- **Pierre Bourdieu**: Cultural capital; habitus; field theory
- **Michel Foucault**: Power/knowledge; discipline; governmentality

## Applications

- **Social Policy**: Welfare, education, housing, healthcare design
- **Criminal Justice**: Policing reform, restorative justice, recidivism reduction
- **Organizational Consulting**: Workplace culture, diversity and inclusion
- **Public Health**: Social determinants of health
- **Market Research**: Consumer behavior, demographic analysis
- **Community Development**: Urban planning, grassroots organizing
