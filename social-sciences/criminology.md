# Criminology

## Overview

Criminology is the interdisciplinary scientific study of crime, criminal behavior, and the criminal justice system. It examines the causes of crime, the nature of criminals, the impact of crime on victims and society, and the effectiveness of crime prevention and control measures. Criminology draws on sociology, psychology, law, economics, and political science to understand crime as a social phenomenon and to develop evidence-based approaches to reducing crime and improving justice.
