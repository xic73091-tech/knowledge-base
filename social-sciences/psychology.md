# Psychology

## Overview

Psychology is the scientific study of the mind and behavior. It encompasses the biological influences, social pressures, and environmental factors that affect how people think, feel, and act. It bridges natural and social sciences, with applications in mental health, education, business, law, and more.

## Core Concepts

### Cognitive Psychology
- **Perception**: Visual, auditory, tactile processing; Gestalt principles
- **Attention**: Selective, divided, sustained; inattentional blindness
- **Memory**: Sensory, short-term (working memory), long-term; encoding, storage, retrieval; forgetting curves (Ebbinghaus)
- **Thinking & Reasoning**: Deductive, inductive; heuristics and biases (Kahneman & Tversky)
- **Language**: Production, comprehension, acquisition (Chomsky's universal grammar)
- **Decision-Making**: Prospect theory; bounded rationality; cognitive biases

### Developmental Psychology
- **Piaget's Stages**: Sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete operational, formal operational
- **Erikson's Psychosocial Stages**: Trust vs mistrust through integrity vs despair
- **Attachment Theory**: Bowlby; secure, anxious, avoidant, disorganized (Ainsworth's Strange Situation)
- **Cognitive Development**: Theory of mind, moral development (Kohlberg), language acquisition
- **Lifespan Development**: Adolescence, adulthood, aging; identity formation

### Social Psychology
- **Social Cognition**: Attribution theory; schemas; stereotyping; prejudice
- **Conformity & Obedience**: Asch conformity; Milgram obedience experiments
- **Group Dynamics**: Social facilitation; deindividuation; groupthink; bystander effect
- **Attitudes & Persuasion**: Cognitive dissonance (Festinger); elaboration likelihood model
- **Prosocial Behavior**: Altruism; empathy; cooperation
- **Aggression**: Biological, social learning, frustration-aggression theories

### Personality Psychology
- **Big Five (OCEAN)**: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism
- **Psychoanalytic Theory**: Freud; id, ego, superego; defense mechanisms; unconscious
- **Humanistic Psychology**: Maslow's hierarchy of needs; self-actualization; Rogers' person-centered approach
- **Trait Theory**: Allport, Cattell; personality as stable characteristics
- **Social-Cognitive Theory**: Bandura; reciprocal determinism; self-efficacy

### Biological Psychology
- **Neurotransmitters**: Dopamine, serotonin, norepinephrine, GABA, glutamate
- **Brain Structure & Function**: Frontal (executive), temporal (memory), parietal (sensation), occipital (vision)
- **Endocrine System**: Hormones and behavior; stress response (HPA axis)
- **Behavioral Genetics**: Twin studies; heritability; gene-environment interaction
- **Neuroimaging**: fMRI, EEG, PET; brain-behavior relationships

### Abnormal Psychology
- **Classification**: DSM-5; ICD; categorical vs dimensional approaches
- **Anxiety Disorders**: Generalized anxiety, phobias, panic disorder, OCD, PTSD
- **Mood Disorders**: Major depression, bipolar disorder
- **Schizophrenia**: Positive (hallucinations, delusions) and negative (flat affect, withdrawal) symptoms
- **Personality Disorders**: Borderline, antisocial, narcissistic
- **Eating Disorders**: Anorexia nervosa, bulimia, binge eating

### Clinical & Counseling Psychology
- **Therapeutic Approaches**: CBT, psychodynamic, humanistic, DBT, EMDR
- **Assessment**: Psychological testing; intelligence (IQ, WAIS); projective tests (Rorschach)
- **Evidence-Based Practice**: Empirically supported treatments
- **Psychotherapy Research**: Effect sizes; common factors; therapeutic alliance

### Research Methods
- **Experimental Design**: Independent/dependent variables; control groups; randomization
- **Correlational Studies**: Correlation vs causation; third variable problem
- **Ethics**: Informed consent, debriefing, IRB review; Milgram/Stanford Prison ethical lessons
- **Statistics**: t-tests, ANOVA, regression; effect sizes; p-values and their limitations

## Key Theories

| Theory | Key Figure | Core Idea |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Psychoanalysis | Freud | Unconscious drives shape behavior |
| Behaviorism | Skinner, Watson | Behavior shaped by reinforcement and punishment |
| Cognitive Revolution | Neisser, Miller | Mental processes can be studied scientifically |
| Humanistic Psychology | Maslow, Rogers | Focus on growth, self-actualization, subjective experience |
| Social Learning | Bandura | Learning through observation and modeling |
| Evolutionary Psychology | Tooby, Cosmides | Mental adaptations shaped by natural selection |

## Important Figures

- **Sigmund Freud**: Psychoanalysis; unconscious mind
- **Carl Jung**: Analytical psychology; archetypes; collective unconscious
- **B.F. Skinner**: Operant conditioning; radical behaviorism
- **Jean Piaget**: Cognitive development in children
- **Abraham Maslow**: Hierarchy of needs; humanistic psychology
- **Albert Bandura**: Social learning theory; self-efficacy; Bobo doll experiment
- **Daniel Kahneman**: Cognitive biases; prospect theory
- **Philip Zimbardo**: Stanford Prison Experiment; situational forces

## Frontiers

- **Replication Crisis and Open Science**: Addressing methodological weaknesses across the field; pre-registration, transparency initiatives, and reproducibility standards
- **Computational Psychiatry**: Using AI and machine learning to model mental disorders, predict treatment outcomes, and personalize therapy
- **Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy**: Renewed clinical research into psilocybin, MDMA, and ketamine for depression, PTSD, and addiction treatment
- **Global Mental Health**: Adapting psychological interventions for diverse cultural contexts; task-shifting and scalability in low-resource settings
- **Consciousness Studies**: The hard problem of consciousness; integrated information theory; competing frameworks for understanding subjective experience

## Applications

- **Mental Health**: Therapy, counseling, psychiatric assessment
- **Education**: Learning strategies, motivation, special education
- **Organizational**: Workplace motivation, leadership, team dynamics
- **Forensic**: Criminal profiling, witness testimony, risk assessment
- **Health**: Health behaviors, chronic illness coping, pain management
- **UX/Design**: User research, cognitive load, interface design
- **Sports**: Performance psychology, motivation, team cohesion

## Classic Works

- **"The Interpretation of Dreams"** by Sigmund Freud — Launched psychoanalysis; introduced the concept of the unconscious mind
- **"Thinking, Fast and Slow"** by Daniel Kahneman — Comprehensive overview of cognitive biases and dual-process theory; reshaped behavioral science
- **"Principles of Psychology"** by William James — Early foundational text of the discipline; functionalist approach to mind and behavior
- **"Obedience to Authority"** by Stanley Milgram — Landmark experimental study on conformity; transformed understanding of social influence
- **"Man's Search for Meaning"** by Viktor Frankl — Introduced logotherapy; enduring account of human resilience and meaning-making under extreme suffering
